# Anti-AI Cleanup

## Goal

Keep the image grounded, materially believable, and free from the common polished AI look.

## Common Failure Modes

- waxy skin, plastic sheen, or over-smoothed surfaces;
- uniform sharpness, texture soup, and equal legibility across the frame;
- decorative clutter, over-designed aging, or glossy shadow detail;
- HDR-like local contrast, synthetic bokeh, poster polish, or cinematic advertising finish;
- cleanly styled dirt, wear, and damage that do not follow use.

## Positive Corrections

Prefer matte surfaces, real material dullness, selective legibility, natural falloff, dim corners, restrained contrast, and non-showy exposure. Only one or two narrative zones should receive full clarity; secondary props may fall into shadow, partial occlusion, soft distance, or incomplete visibility. Do not evenly expose faces, walls, cardboard, furniture, and background architecture. Allow ordinary surfaces to become visually dull, unresolved, or unattractive, and preserve dead tonal areas where practical light does not reach.

Let texture appear unevenly where light, distance, and focus permit it. Keep wear causal and lived-in; do not beautify poverty, age, wear, domestic clutter, or damaged interiors, and do not turn ordinary wear into production-designed texture. Use blur or softness only when the camera, movement, or optics justify it. Do not use pleasing window light as a substitute for material realism. Avoid globally smooth skin and uniformly readable facial features. Build atmosphere from space and tone, not polish.

## Output Reminder

Do not make every prop equally clear or every wall attractive. Do not turn old into carefully designed old. Do not use grain as a surface filter, or high sharpness and local contrast as a substitute for material truth.

## Final Check

If the frame still looks like a carefully art-directed lifestyle advertisement, reduce polish, local contrast, prop legibility, and highlight beauty before answering.
