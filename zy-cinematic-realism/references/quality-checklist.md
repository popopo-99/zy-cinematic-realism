# Quality Checklist

Before responding, confirm every applicable item:

- [ ] The frame has a clear place in a story, with a trace of before or after.
- [ ] Each visible character performs a concrete, natural action rather than posing.
- [ ] The environment contains a small number of causal, story-relevant traces.
- [ ] Foreground, midground, and background relationships are physically plausible.
- [ ] The camera position, distance, and height can be sketched in the location.
- [ ] Occlusion or reflection, if present, follows from that camera position.
- [ ] Composition supports the emotion without defaulting to symmetry or centered promotion.
- [ ] Every important light has a source in the scene.
- [ ] Color follows the sources, time, weather, and materials.
- [ ] Film, lens, grain, and halation reinforce rather than replace the scene.
- [ ] The prompt remains specific after removing generic cinematic and camera-brand words.
- [ ] The Avoid list is relevant, concise, and non-contradictory.
- [ ] Period, wardrobe, vehicles, architecture, signage, and props are consistent.
- [ ] No required user fact or restriction was silently changed.
- [ ] The output contains no placeholders or hidden reasoning.

If the frame reads like an advertisement, poster, fashion portrait, concept sheet, or game render, rewrite the subject scale, action, camera placement, and light logic before adding more style words.
