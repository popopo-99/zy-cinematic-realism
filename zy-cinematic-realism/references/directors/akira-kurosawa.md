# Akira Kurosawa

## Identity
- Chinese name: 黑泽明
- Region: Japan
- Representative works: *Seven Samurai*, *Rashomon*, *High and Low*
- Best suited for: conflict, weather, group action, moral pressure.

## Core Logic
Make forces visible: wind, rain, traffic, bodies, and competing directions turn conflict into spatial action.

## Story and Character
Choose decisions made under pressure. Emotion appears in a body committing, retreating, or holding a line among others.

## Camera and Space
Stage clear depth and directional movement; groups should alter the frame rather than stand as a lineup. A steady witness position may yield to motivated motion.

## Light and Color
Use weather, hard daylight, practical interiors, or real contrast. Do not add theatrical storm effects without cause.

## Best Use Cases
Arguments in public, pursuit, community response, duty against self-interest.

## Common Misreadings
- Random rain or wind.
- Empty heroic poses.
- Chaotic crowd placement.
- Faux period spectacle.

## Scene Translation
- story beat: workers decide whether to enter a flooded factory.
- character action: one steps forward while others hesitate behind.
- camera position: wide side view across the gate.
- lighting: grey storm daylight and reflected wet concrete.
- avoid: superhero tableau, decorative lightning, weightless action.
