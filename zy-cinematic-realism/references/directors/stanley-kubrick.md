# Stanley Kubrick

## Identity
- Chinese name: 斯坦利·库布里克
- Region: United States / United Kingdom
- Representative works: *2001: A Space Odyssey*, *The Shining*, *Barry Lyndon*
- Best suited for: ritual, institutional systems, geometry, controlled unease.

## Core Dramatic Logic
Choose routine becoming psychologically charged. Formal order makes a person's unstable position inside an institution, procedure, or room legible.

## Light and Contrast Fingerprint
- Source hierarchy: use credible practical, daylight, period lamps, or institutional fixtures arranged with formal consistency.
- Shadow and contrast: keep contrast disciplined and structural; broad lit planes meet deliberate recesses rather than expressionistic chaos.
- Highlights and visibility: practicals and architectural surfaces may be exact and bright while human faces remain coolly integrated.
- Reject: automatic horror red, decorative spotlighting, glossy sci-fi glow, or arbitrary high contrast.

## Color and Exposure Fingerprint
- Palette and saturation: use restrained but exact color relationships tied to production space, uniforms, décor, and source temperature.
- Skin and midtones: keep skin controlled within the room's formal palette rather than warming it for empathy.
- Blacks, whites, exposure: protect formal whites and lamp highlights, retain measured blacks, and avoid HDR expansion.
- Reject: sterile blue filter, copied red motifs, period sepia, or color coding without spatial function.

## Lens and Camera Fingerprint
- Distance and lens: use deliberate wide or normal perspective that makes spatial geometry and body placement undeniable.
- Height and position: align the camera with a real architectural axis at a measured standing, seated, or corridor height.
- Movement and focus: use locked precision, controlled tracking, or slow axial movement; keep measured depth and avoid casual focus breathing.
- Witness role: an impersonal institutional observer, not a playful decorator; reject arbitrary extreme wide distortion and restless coverage.

## Composition and Spatial Fingerprint
- Visual center: place human instability against order; centered framing is optional, not mandatory.
- Layers and obstruction: architecture, repeated fixtures, furniture, and thresholds define pressure with minimal clutter.
- Blocking and geometry: ritualize gestures and distances, using repetition, frontal planes, or central perspective only when the location supports them.
- Information control: expose the system clearly while leaving motive and emotion coolly unresolved.

## Blocking and Story Translation
- Moment: choose a rule being followed one beat too long or a routine that starts to fail.
- Character action: align, wait, repeat, inspect, or violate a small procedure.
- Visual center: make order compete with one human irregularity.
- Blocking: use measured spacing and controlled axes.
- Environment: let architecture and repetition enforce behavior.

## Capture Texture
Use clean, materially credible capture, disciplined exposure, stable depth, and exact surfaces without digital gloss. Precision should feel coldly observed, not synthetic.

## Common Misreadings
- Centering every subject automatically.
- Copying one-point corridor perspective.
- Turning all scenes into horror.
- Treating actors as emotionless mannequins.
- Using extreme wide angles or sterile sci-fi as shortcuts.

## Nearest-Neighbor Contrast
Unlike Wes Anderson, Kubrick uses order as existential and institutional pressure: cooler formal source light, restrained exact color, psychologically distancing lens geometry, and ritualized bodies threatened by the system rather than storybook palette, lateral dollhouse staging, playful flatness, and handcrafted visual wit.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor
Use deliberate wide-or-normal perspective, formal motivated light, restrained exact color, measured depth, ritualized blocking, controlled architecture, and visual order strained by one unstable human action. Do not default to symmetry.

## Default Iconic Anchor
Reselect the scene as a routine becoming unsettling; align a physically possible camera with the room's governing axis, stage measured gestures against repeated architecture, preserve formal light and exact color, and let one irregular body disturb order without corridor cliché.

## Scene Translation
- Scene: a trainee waits outside a review room.
- Translation: she aligns a folder with the floor seam then stops; a measured wide witness uses repeated doors and ceiling fixtures to hold her small procedural break under flat institutional light.
