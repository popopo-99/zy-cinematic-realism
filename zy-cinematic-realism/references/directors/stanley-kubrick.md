# Stanley Kubrick

## Identity
- Chinese name: 斯坦利·库布里克
- Region: United States / United Kingdom
- Representative works: *2001: A Space Odyssey*, *The Shining*, *Barry Lyndon*
- Best suited for: systems, ritual, geometry, controlled distance.

## Core Logic
Use order to expose pressure. The frame makes the character’s place within an institution, room, or procedure legible.

## Story and Character
Choose routine becoming unsettling, a rule being observed, or someone made small by a system. Keep action precise.

## Camera and Space
Use clear axes, measured distance, and architectural repetition where the actual location supports them. Do not force central symmetry.

## Light and Color
Use credible practical, daylight, or period sources; discipline exposure instead of adding stylized contrast.

## Best Use Cases
Hospitals, schools, military or corporate systems, formal interiors.

## Common Misreadings
- Mandatory centered framing.
- Horror lighting everywhere.
- Sterile sci-fi.
- Emotionless mannequins.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor

- Explicit director token: Stanley Kubrick
- Representative film anchors: *2001: A Space Odyssey*, *The Shining*, *Barry Lyndon*
- Strong visual grammar: deliberate wide or normal-lens perspective and controlled geometry; ritualized blocking against psychologically pressurized architecture; cool observational distance; formal, motivated light logic; restrained, exact color; measured depth and focus; clean but materially credible capture; visual order held against human instability.
- Do not reduce to: automatic symmetry, corridors, extreme wide angles, red bathrooms, or a copied one-point perspective.
