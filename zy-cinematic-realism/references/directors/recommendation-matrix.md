# Director Recommendation Matrix

Use this matrix only when the user asks for a recommendation or has not chosen a director. Recommend two or three candidates, explain the contrast, and then load only the selected files. Do not average fingerprints: each option is a distinct direction.

| Scene goal | First choice | Second choice | Four-axis difference | Why not mix by default |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Urban loneliness | [Wong Kar-wai](wong-kar-wai.md) | [Tsai Ming-liang](tsai-ming-liang.md) | Wong uses broken mixed practicals, contaminated color, compressed/reflective proximity, and incomplete encounters; Tsai uses dead fixed light, isolated worn color, a locked wide camera, and vacancy around routine. | Reflective subjective compression contradicts severe static vacancy. |
| Family relationships | [Hirokazu Kore-eda](hirokazu-koreeda.md) | [Yasujiro Ozu](yasujiro-ozu.md) | Kore-eda uses flexible household light, warm-neutral color, eye-level reframing, and overlapping care; Ozu uses gentle stable light, quiet exact color, low locked optics, and meaningful empty planes. | Mobile behavioral overlap weakens Ozu's formal aftermath. |
| Crime investigation | [David Fincher](david-fincher.md) | [Diao Yinan](diao-yinan.md) | Fincher builds functional low light, neutral exposure, exact information focus, and causal work-surface geometry; Diao uses isolated public practical pools, dirty color, delayed medium-wide observation, and awkward danger inside routine. | Procedural clarity and fatalistic public fragmentation pull in opposite directions. |
| Heavy rain and natural pressure | [Akira Kurosawa](akira-kurosawa.md) | [Alfonso Cuarón](alfonso-cuaron.md) | Kurosawa uses hard weather contrast, material color, action-axis camera, and opposed multi-plane blocking; Cuarón lets exposure evolve along a continuous route, preserves location color, and migrates attention through public consequence. | Forceful action geometry conflicts with mobile social continuity. |
| Youth and memory | [Shunji Iwai](shunji-iwai.md) | [Bi Gan](bi-gan.md) | Iwai uses pale seasonal light, gentle source bloom, intimate normal-lens distance, and a fragile object-afterimage; Bi uses humid practical bloom, contaminated color, immersive continuous movement, and nested temporal routes. | Delicate still afterimage and route-driven temporal immersion compete for the visual center. |
| Institutional space | [Stanley Kubrick](stanley-kubrick.md) | [Denis Villeneuve](denis-villeneuve.md) | Kubrick uses formal source order, exact color, deliberate geometry, and ritualized bodies; Villeneuve uses broad heavy contrast, restrained material color, measured wide distance, and sparse figures under oppressive scale. | Ritual precision and environmental withholding create different power models. |
| Science-fiction scale | [Denis Villeneuve](denis-villeneuve.md) | [Christopher Nolan](christopher-nolan.md) | Villeneuve makes severe space and silence dominate; Nolan makes practical mechanisms, neutral exposure, participant camera, and intersecting routes carry urgency. | Environmental awe and causal mechanism should remain separate directions. |
| Historical epic | [Zhang Yimou](zhang-yimou.md) | [King Hu](king-hu.md) | Zhang uses dominant color masses, ceremonial contrast, measured axes, and repeated collective order; King Hu uses readable threshold light, restrained materials, grounded wide geography, and tactical entrances. | Graphic mass ritual can obscure King Hu's tactical spatial clarity. |
| Dream and psychological anomaly | [David Lynch](david-lynch.md) | [Bi Gan](bi-gan.md) | Lynch uses loaded practical pools, psychological color, creeping or discontinuous camera, and reality seams; Bi uses humid source bloom, walking continuity, focus adaptation, and temporal uncertainty inside physical routes. | Subjective causal rupture conflicts with continuous traversable dream space. |
| Long-duration observation | [Hou Hsiao-hsien](hou-hsiao-hsien.md) | [Tsai Ming-liang](tsai-ming-liang.md) | Hou keeps restrained inhabited light, muted color, medium-long multi-plane life, and partial social information; Tsai fixes dead light and worn color around isolated repetitive bodies and vacancy. | Layered social duration and severe empty duration need different blocking. |
| Action choreography | [King Hu](king-hu.md) | [Akira Kurosawa](akira-kurosawa.md) | King Hu maps thresholds, clear source light, grounded optics, and precise entrances; Kurosawa uses harder weather contrast, material force, responsive camera, and colliding movement across depth. | Tactical concealment and weather-driven collision should not be averaged. |
| Romantic misalignment | [Wong Kar-wai](wong-kar-wai.md) | [Lou Ye](lou-ye.md) | Wong composes mixed practical color, reflective compression, and selective smear; Lou lets available light break, color remain restless, handheld access fail, and bodies collide with crowds. | Composed emotional delay and physically uncontrolled intimacy are distinct witness roles. |
| Absurd comedy | [Wes Anderson](wes-anderson.md) | [Bong Joon-ho](bong-joon-ho.md) | Wes uses soft graphic light, limited object color, orthogonal optics, and a misfit gesture in precise order; Bong uses functional contrast, social color zones, wide ensemble geography, and a status reversal. | Handcrafted comic order can neutralize Bong's class geography and tonal threat. |
| Horror and invisible threat | [Kiyoshi Kurosawa](kiyoshi-kurosawa.md) | [Robert Eggers](robert-eggers.md) | Kiyoshi uses flat ordinary light, drained color, calm wide rooms, and one causal void; Eggers uses source-poor historical light, restricted earth color, severe stable optics, and belief under elemental pressure. | Contemporary spatial absence and period belief require incompatible source maps. |
| Modern architecture and alienation | [Edward Yang](edward-yang.md) | [Denis Villeneuve](denis-villeneuve.md) | Yang maps relationships with ordinary practical contrast, neutral urban color, lucid medium distance, and windows/corridors; Villeneuve uses broad oppressive light, restrained color, measured wides, and negative space that dwarfs people. | Relational social mapping and monumental environmental pressure should remain separate options. |

When a user asks to mix, keep the first choice primary. Assign the secondary director exactly one axis, verify source and camera compatibility, and state the axis explicitly.
