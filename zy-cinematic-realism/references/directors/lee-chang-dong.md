# Lee Chang-dong

## Identity
- Chinese name: 李沧东
- Region: South Korea
- Representative works: *Secret Sunshine*, *Poetry*, *Burning*
- Best suited for: moral injury, social ambiguity, grief, ordinary life under unseen pressure.

## Core Dramatic Logic
Choose an ordinary action after a moral or emotional rupture. Let performance, social setting, and withheld knowledge coexist until a small gesture exposes contradiction.

## Light and Contrast Fingerprint
- Source hierarchy: use daylight, apartment windows, shops, classrooms, churches, fields, and plain practical interiors.
- Shadow and contrast: keep natural moderate contrast, allowing a face or room to become briefly harsh when circumstance changes.
- Highlights and visibility: sun, pale sky, cheap interiors, and ordinary reflective surfaces remain unromantic.
- Reject: grief backlight, elegant noir, spiritual glow, or darkness used to manufacture ambiguity.

## Color and Exposure Fingerprint
- Palette and saturation: preserve ordinary Korean streets, skin, clothing, vegetation, interiors, and seasonal weather without beautification.
- Skin and midtones: keep skin emotionally exposed but naturally embedded in ambient light.
- Blacks, whites, exposure: use honest midtones, occasional harsh whites, and unforced blacks; tolerate mundane exposure.
- Reject: tasteful melancholy grade, green social realism, golden redemption color, or muted prestige palette.

## Lens and Camera Fingerprint
- Distance and lens: use normal or mildly long medium observation, with wider context when social setting complicates the subject.
- Height and position: stay at ordinary eye level in rooms, streets, fields, or public gatherings.
- Movement and focus: hold patiently or reframe minimally; focus remains contextual and does not announce meaning.
- Witness role: an ethically attentive observer who cannot resolve judgment, not a sentimental confidant; reject expressive camera consolation.

## Composition and Spatial Fingerprint
- Visual center: place a vulnerable gesture within an ordinary social context that may contradict it.
- Layers and obstruction: use doorways, traffic, neighbors, gatherings, fields, and household objects without pictorial emphasis.
- Blocking and geometry: let people interrupt, misunderstand, watch, or continue routine around the subject.
- Information control: reveal behavior clearly while withholding motive, guilt, or definitive interpretation.

## Blocking and Story Translation
- Moment: choose the ordinary task after rupture rather than the rupture itself.
- Character action: write, clean, wait, attend, look, or continue work.
- Visual center: privilege contradiction between gesture and context.
- Blocking: keep bystanders or social routine present.
- Environment: let mundane life resist emotional closure.

## Capture Texture
Use natural skin, ordinary daylight, plain interiors, stable focus, and unembellished temporal patience. Avoid poetic polish and visual pity.

## Common Misreadings
- Turning grief into beauty.
- Using ambiguity as empty mystery.
- Isolating the protagonist from social context.
- Adding sentimental close-ups.
- Applying muted prestige color.

## Nearest-Neighbor Contrast
Unlike Hirokazu Kore-eda, Lee Chang-dong is more morally abrasive and isolating: harder ordinary daylight, less comforting color, patient medium distance, and contradictory social context around a solitary gesture rather than flexible household light, warm-neutral materials, overlapping family blocking, and care distributed across routines.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor
Use plain source light, ordinary ungraded color, patient eye-level medium observation, a vulnerable action inside contradictory social context, contextual focus, and moral ambiguity without visual consolation.

## Default Iconic Anchor
Reselect the mundane action after emotional rupture; hold an ethically attentive camera at ordinary distance, keep bystanders or routine active, use honest daylight or practical exposure, and let one gesture remain clear while motive and judgment stay unresolved.

## Scene Translation
- Scene: a teacher returns to class after learning a former student has disappeared.
- Translation: she erases yesterday's assignment while students enter and talk behind her; an eye-level rear-room view keeps chalk dust, white window light, and her stopped hand within otherwise ordinary activity.
