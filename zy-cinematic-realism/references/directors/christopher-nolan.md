# Christopher Nolan

## Identity
- Chinese name: 克里斯托弗·诺兰
- Region: United Kingdom / United States
- Representative works: *Inception*, *Interstellar*, *Dunkirk*
- Best suited for: time pressure, mechanisms, physical stakes.

## Core Logic
Make the constraint physical and immediate: a clock, route, machine, weather event, or limited space forces a choice.

## Story and Character
Choose the instant before commitment or amid a task. A person remains human inside the larger event through a concrete action.

## Camera and Space
Keep geography readable and scale grounded. Use movement only when it clarifies urgency or mechanics.

## Light and Color
Use work lights, weather, dashboard glow, daylight, or emergency sources that the setting contains.

## Best Use Cases
Rescue, transit, technical work, separated teams, impending consequences.

## Common Misreadings
- Abstract time graphics.
- Explosions without story cause.
- Weightless spectacle.
- Impossible architecture.

- Explicit director token: Christopher Nolan
