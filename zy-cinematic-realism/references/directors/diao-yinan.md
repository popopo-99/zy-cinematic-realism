# Diao Yinan

## Identity
- Chinese name: 刁亦男
- Region: China
- Representative works: *Black Coal, Thin Ice*, *The Wild Goose Lake*, *Night Train*
- Best suited for: crime investigation, nocturnal labor spaces, fatal encounters, social coldness.

## Core Dramatic Logic
Choose an off-angle encounter in which routine labor, crime, and private desire intersect. Information arrives through awkward bodies and indifferent public space rather than polished detective procedure.

## Light and Contrast Fingerprint
- Source hierarchy: use street lamps, shop fluorescents, vehicle lights, dance-hall practicals, factory fixtures, or winter daylight.
- Shadow and contrast: create isolated hard pools within broad indifferent darkness; let barriers and weather cut visibility.
- Highlights and visibility: wet asphalt, snow, tile, metal, and cheap synthetic fabric catch sharp, source-specific highlights.
- Reject: elegant noir key light, glossy rain, universal cyan darkness, or beautiful silhouette lighting.

## Color and Exposure Fingerprint
- Palette and saturation: allow dirty greens, sodium amber, weak magenta, cold blue, and industrial neutrals only where actual sources and materials provide them.
- Skin and midtones: keep skin pale, tired, contaminated, or partly underexposed rather than attractively separated.
- Blacks, whites, exposure: tolerate hard practical clipping, dense exterior blacks, dead midtones, and abrupt source transitions.
- Reject: uniform neon-noir grade, teal-orange polish, tasteful desaturation, or romantic color harmony.

## Lens and Camera Fingerprint
- Distance and lens: use medium-wide or mildly compressed observation that keeps awkward action and public space connected.
- Height and position: watch from across a road, beside a platform, behind glass, or at a room edge where the witness feels slightly late.
- Movement and focus: hold unexpectedly, pan after action begins, or track with restrained bluntness; focus may favor the wrong social detail before correcting.
- Witness role: a cool bystander in an unsafe public space, not an omniscient detective; reject slick procedural moves and frenetic handheld coverage.

## Composition and Spatial Fingerprint
- Visual center: place a small suspicious gesture inside a larger labor, leisure, or transit environment.
- Layers and obstruction: use windows, snow, rain, crowds, bicycles, tables, industrial frames, and road space.
- Blocking and geometry: keep bodies awkwardly separated, entering from edges or colliding without heroic clarity.
- Information control: reveal the consequence before fully explaining the cause; let public activity continue around danger.

## Blocking and Story Translation
- Moment: choose the second after recognition but before pursuit becomes explicit.
- Character action: conceal, wait, cross, discard, or glance toward an exit.
- Visual center: privilege the compromised gesture rather than the clue itself.
- Blocking: isolate people within active public space and unequal sight lines.
- Environment: make weather, transit, or work routine impede certainty.

## Capture Texture
Use blunt practical exposure, wet or winter material response, selective sharpness, and cold social distance. Avoid glossy neo-noir finish and decorative grit.

## Common Misreadings
- Turning every scene into neon rain.
- Styling criminals as cool heroes.
- Copying a green-cyan noir grade.
- Making investigation too procedurally neat.
- Using snow or wet streets without changing movement.

## Nearest-Neighbor Contrast
Unlike David Fincher, Diao lets procedure leak into awkward public life: harsher isolated practical pools, dirtier source color, more delayed medium-wide observation, and fragmented social blocking rather than exact functional light, restrained neutral exposure, controlled camera placement, and causal evidence chains.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor
Use an awkward encounter inside nocturnal labor or transit space, isolated hard practical pools, dirty source color, delayed medium-wide observation, edge-driven blocking, and public routine continuing around partially understood danger.

## Default Iconic Anchor
Reselect the moment just after recognition; place a cool witness slightly too far or late, let one compromised gesture occupy a hard practical pool while public space remains dark and active, and keep color dirty, bodies awkward, and causal information incomplete.

## Scene Translation
- Scene: a young police officer searches a blackout video hall for a missing person's tape.
- Translation: he notices a cassette changing hands near the exit after emergency power returns; a medium-wide aisle witness uses one green tube, red exit light, and rainy doorway spill while patrons continue leaving across his sight line.
