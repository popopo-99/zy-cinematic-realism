# Michael Mann

## Identity
- Chinese name: 迈克尔·曼
- Region: United States
- Representative works: *Heat*, *The Insider*, *Collateral*
- Best suited for: professional systems, nocturnal cities, surveillance, procedure under personal cost.

## Core Dramatic Logic
Choose a professional action executed with precision while personal consequence enters at the edge. City infrastructure and technology become real working environments, not futuristic decoration.

## Light and Contrast Fingerprint
- Source hierarchy: use sodium streets, mercury or LED spill, office fluorescents, vehicle dashboards, airport or industrial fixtures, and early digital low-light response.
- Shadow and contrast: build broad city darkness punctuated by real infrastructure sources; faces may be uneven but tasks remain legible.
- Highlights and visibility: glass towers, wet roads, metal, screens, headlights, and skin catch hard urban highlights.
- Reject: cyberpunk neon, glossy blue rim light, universal darkness, or luxury-car commercial lighting.

## Color and Exposure Fingerprint
- Palette and saturation: use mixed urban cyan, sodium amber, green fluorescent, concrete, steel, and night sky according to actual sources.
- Skin and midtones: allow source contamination and digital low-light coarseness without plastic correction.
- Blacks, whites, exposure: tolerate noisy or thin blacks, clipped practicals, open night midtones, and source-driven white-balance conflict.
- Reject: teal-orange action grade, magenta cyberpunk, polished night blue, or smooth cinematic black.

## Lens and Camera Fingerprint
- Distance and lens: combine precise medium professional observation, long-lens surveillance, and mobile wide or normal proximity during operations.
- Height and position: occupy vehicles, rooftops, offices, streets, terminals, or tactical routes at actual working positions.
- Movement and focus: track efficiently with procedure, use long-lens compression across city space, and retain enough focus for infrastructure.
- Witness role: a skilled professional aware of systems and risk, not a glamorous action spectator; reject impossible chase cameras.

## Composition and Spatial Fingerprint
- Visual center: connect a professional gesture to a network, route, city grid, or approaching personal consequence.
- Layers and obstruction: use glass, traffic, vehicles, monitors, fences, roads, and architectural grids.
- Blocking and geometry: stage teams and solitary figures through operational lines, cross-city separation, and timed movement.
- Information control: make the task clear while keeping emotional cost at the edge until it intrudes.

## Blocking and Story Translation
- Moment: choose competent execution just as personal risk becomes visible.
- Character action: monitor, drive, load, wait, call, align, or abort.
- Visual center: bind person to tool and urban system.
- Blocking: keep professional routes exact and separated.
- Environment: let traffic, towers, terminals, or industrial networks constrain timing.

## Capture Texture
Use real nocturnal source contamination, glass, steel, road reflections, digital low-light grain or natural film response, and practical operational detail. Avoid luxury advertising.

## Common Misreadings
- Calling all blue night scenes Michael Mann-like.
- Turning cities into cyberpunk.
- Glamorizing cars and weapons.
- Hiding procedure in shallow focus.
- Smoothing digital low-light texture.

## Nearest-Neighbor Contrast
Unlike David Fincher, Mann expands procedure into live urban networks: harsher mixed infrastructure light, more open source-conflicted color, mobile and long-lens city observation, and operational routes across distance rather than controlled low illumination, restrained neutral color, exact room-bound camera, and evidence chains organized on work surfaces.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor
Use real mixed city sources, source-conflicted night color, professional camera positions, mobile or long-lens operational observation, infrastructure depth, and precise work disrupted by personal consequence.

## Default Iconic Anchor
Reselect competent action as personal risk enters the frame; place the camera on the actual operational route, keep tool, city network, and timing legible, accept mixed-source night exposure and coarse blacks, and refuse cyberpunk or luxury polish.

## Scene Translation
- Scene: an airport technician discovers a familiar name on a cargo manifest.
- Translation: he keeps scanning containers while calling from the tug lane; a long-lens terminal view compresses sodium ramps, cyan hangar light, moving loaders, and his still figure against the operational flow.
