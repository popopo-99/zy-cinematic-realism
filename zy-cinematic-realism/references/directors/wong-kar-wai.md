# Wong Kar-wai

## Identity
- Chinese name: 王家卫
- Region: Hong Kong
- Representative works: *In the Mood for Love*, *Chungking Express*, *Happy Together*
- Best suited for: urban solitude, missed connection, temporal dislocation.

## Core Logic
Treat a city as a series of near-encounters. Feeling is held in interruption, reflection, repeated routes, and the space people leave between themselves.

## Story and Character
Choose waiting, passing, or almost speaking. Let hands, glances, and routine tasks carry intimacy without confession.

## Camera and Space
Use plausible layers—doorways, mirrors, crowds, counter edges—and a close or mid-distance witness. Motion may blur only when the location motivates it.

## Light and Color
Use actual shop, hallway, street, or window sources. Saturation must follow available light, not become neon decoration.

## Best Use Cases
Night shifts, apartment corridors, rain delays, unspoken relationships.

## Common Misreadings
- Neon everywhere.
- Random slow shutter blur.
- Glamour portraiture.
- Decorative sensuality.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor

- Explicit director token: Wong Kar-wai
- Representative film anchors: *In the Mood for Love*, *Chungking Express*, *Fallen Angels*
- Strong visual grammar: close or compressed urban observation interrupted by frames, glass, and reflections; bodies crossing incomplete encounters; longer-lens compression or motivated wide-angle distortion; mixed practical city light; color born from real signage, rooms, and street sources; selective motion smear and focus isolation; tactile, time-dislocated capture; subjective longing rather than decorative atmosphere.
- Do not reduce to: neon, smoking, green filters, slow shutter blur, cheongsam, or generic romance.
- Prompt insertion: Director reference: Wong Kar-wai, with broad visual reference points in *In the Mood for Love*, *Chungking Express*, and *Fallen Angels*. Use fragmented urban intimacy, obstructed and reflective framing, compressed or distorted spatial relationships, subjective time, incomplete encounters, motivated mixed city light, selective motion smear, and color shaped by real practical sources rather than a generic neon filter.

## Default Iconic Anchor

Director and visual reference: Wong Kar-wai, drawing strongly from the visual language associated with In the Mood for Love, Chungking Express, and Fallen Angels. Reselect the moment around an incomplete urban encounter or interrupted private gesture; use obstructed and reflective framing, compressed or slightly distorted proximity, bodies separated by frames and passing light, selective motion smear, shallow or split focus only where physical movement supports it, and color made by mixed practical city sources rather than a generic neon wash. Keep the capture tactile, partial, and emotionally delayed instead of polished.

## Scene Translation
- story beat: two tenants arrive home seconds apart and do not speak.
- character action: one holds the lift door, then lets it close.
- camera position: across the corridor behind a laundry rack.
- lighting: tired fluorescent ceiling light and spill from apartments.
- avoid: cyberpunk neon, fashion pose, romantic montage.
