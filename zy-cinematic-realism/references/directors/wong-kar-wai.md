# Wong Kar-wai

## Identity
- Chinese name: 王家卫
- Region: Hong Kong
- Representative works: *In the Mood for Love*, *Chungking Express*, *Happy Together*
- Best suited for: urban solitude, missed connection, temporal dislocation.

## Core Logic
Treat a city as a series of near-encounters. Feeling is held in interruption, reflection, repeated routes, and the space people leave between themselves.

## Story and Character
Choose waiting, passing, or almost speaking. Let hands, glances, and routine tasks carry intimacy without confession.

## Camera and Space
Use plausible layers—doorways, mirrors, crowds, counter edges—and a close or mid-distance witness. Motion may blur only when the location motivates it.

## Light and Color
Use actual shop, hallway, street, or window sources. Saturation must follow available light, not become neon decoration.

## Best Use Cases
Night shifts, apartment corridors, rain delays, unspoken relationships.

## Common Misreadings
- Neon everywhere.
- Random slow shutter blur.
- Glamour portraiture.
- Decorative sensuality.

## Scene Translation
- story beat: two tenants arrive home seconds apart and do not speak.
- character action: one holds the lift door, then lets it close.
- camera position: across the corridor behind a laundry rack.
- lighting: tired fluorescent ceiling light and spill from apartments.
- avoid: cyberpunk neon, fashion pose, romantic montage.
