# Lou Ye

## Identity
- Chinese name: 娄烨
- Region: China
- Representative works: *Suzhou River*, *Summer Palace*, *Blind Massage*
- Best suited for: unstable intimacy, urban desire, political pressure, bodies in motion.

## Core Dramatic Logic
Choose the instant emotion disrupts a person's planned action. The camera stays physically implicated as bodies, crowds, rooms, and weather make intimacy unstable.

## Light and Contrast Fingerprint
- Source hierarchy: rely on available windows, fluorescents, street lamps, bars, headlights, and overcast daylight.
- Shadow and contrast: accept abrupt changes, blocked faces, flare, and uneven local contrast as the camera struggles through real space.
- Highlights and visibility: practicals may clip or streak; skin appears and disappears rather than receiving a controlled key.
- Reject: arranged neon beauty, stable romance lighting, glossy darkness, or expressive light without camera obstruction.

## Color and Exposure Fingerprint
- Palette and saturation: keep mixed urban source color restless and sometimes dirty, with no single elegant palette.
- Skin and midtones: allow imperfect skin exposure, source contamination, and movement-driven shifts.
- Blacks, whites, exposure: tolerate crushed passages, clipped practicals, changing white balance, and rough adaptation when physically caused.
- Reject: Wong-style decorative mixed color, teal-orange grit, uniform green cast, or polished film emulation.

## Lens and Camera Fingerprint
- Distance and lens: use close normal-to-wide proximity or crowded medium views that preserve bodily pressure.
- Height and position: remain shoulder-close, behind bodies, in doorways, vehicles, crowds, or cramped rooms.
- Movement and focus: use restless handheld following, abrupt reframing, occlusion, focus lag, and physically earned blur.
- Witness role: an implicated companion who can lose sight of the subject; reject elegant floating motion and detached long-lens romance.

## Composition and Spatial Fingerprint
- Visual center: let a body, touch, or loss of contact displace the planned subject.
- Layers and obstruction: use shoulders, crowds, glass, doorways, vehicles, and walls as collisions, not decorative frames.
- Blocking and geometry: people cross camera, separate unpredictably, and pull focus through unstable space.
- Information control: allow faces and events to become incomplete because the witness cannot maintain access.

## Blocking and Story Translation
- Moment: choose the instant a private impulse interrupts public behavior.
- Character action: follow, turn, push through, withdraw, touch, or lose someone.
- Visual center: privilege the bodily rupture over environmental beauty.
- Blocking: keep other bodies capable of blocking or redirecting the camera.
- Environment: make crowds, rain, traffic, or cramped rooms change access.

## Capture Texture
Use physically stressed handheld motion, focus lag, source flare, uneven exposure, and bodily proximity. Avoid applying grime or blur as a universal surface effect.

## Common Misreadings
- Treating all handheld footage as expressive.
- Adding random blur and flare.
- Styling urban desire as neon romance.
- Making camera instability spatially impossible.
- Confusing rough exposure with low-quality texture.

## Nearest-Neighbor Contrast
Unlike Wong Kar-wai, Lou Ye sacrifices pictorial control to bodily access: light changes more abruptly, color is less patterned, the close handheld camera can lose its subject, and composition is broken by collision rather than arranged through reflective compression and selective temporal smear.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor
Use implicated shoulder-close observation, unstable available light, restless mixed color, handheld following, focus lag, bodily obstruction, and a private impulse disrupting public action. Do not polish instability into neon romance.

## Default Iconic Anchor
Reselect the instant emotion breaks the planned action; keep a physically possible camera close enough to be blocked or redirected, allow focus and exposure to lag through available sources, and make the lost bodily connection—not atmospheric city color—the visual center.

## Scene Translation
- Scene: two former lovers see each other across a crowded hospital corridor.
- Translation: one turns before speaking and the camera is pushed behind passing nurses; fluorescent light clips on white coats, focus catches a shoulder instead of the face, and the distance closes then breaks.
