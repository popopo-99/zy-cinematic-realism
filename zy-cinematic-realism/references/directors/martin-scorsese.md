# Martin Scorsese

## Identity
- Chinese name: 马丁·斯科塞斯
- Region: United States
- Representative works: *Taxi Driver*, *Goodfellas*, *Raging Bull*
- Best suited for: volatile identity, street institutions, temptation, guilt, social performance.

## Core Dramatic Logic
Choose the instant a character performs confidence while appetite, shame, or danger leaks through behavior. Social spaces judge and energize the person at once.

## Light and Contrast Fingerprint
- Source hierarchy: use bars, street lamps, headlights, restaurants, dressing rooms, apartments, arenas, and church or club practicals.
- Shadow and contrast: allow nervous pools, hard source transitions, reflected street light, and localized darkness around social pressure.
- Highlights and visibility: sweat, mirrors, chrome, smoke from real rooms, glass, and eyes may flash within restless environments.
- Reject: generic gangster low key, glossy neon, constant red light, or shadow detached from social place.

## Color and Exposure Fingerprint
- Palette and saturation: use volatile source color—street red, bar amber, fluorescent green, flash white, restaurant warmth—without a uniform grade.
- Skin and midtones: let skin shift with appetite, heat, fatigue, and social setting.
- Blacks, whites, exposure: tolerate hot practicals, firm blacks, and changing midtones while reactions stay readable.
- Reject: teal-orange crime grade, nostalgic Italian warmth, desaturated grit, or saturated color without behavioral pressure.

## Lens and Camera Fingerprint
- Distance and lens: use assertive wide or normal proximity, subjective longer-lens observation, and closer frames when performance cracks.
- Height and position: move through street, club, apartment, car, kitchen, or arena at participant height.
- Movement and focus: track, pan, whip, or hold with behavioral motivation; use focus and speed to shift complicity.
- Witness role: an implicated social participant, not a solemn institutional observer; reject empty bravura and random handheld frenzy.

## Composition and Spatial Fingerprint
- Visual center: place the performed self against judging faces, mirrors, or a social route.
- Layers and obstruction: use tables, bars, cars, mirrors, crowds, doors, smoke, and street traffic.
- Blocking and geometry: let status appear through entrances, seating, who follows whom, and who commands movement.
- Information control: reveal seductive access before exposing cost or isolation.

## Blocking and Story Translation
- Moment: choose the instant confidence overreaches or private guilt interrupts performance.
- Character action: enter, boast, watch, rehearse, follow, lash out, or withdraw.
- Visual center: privilege the crack between self-image and social reality.
- Blocking: keep witnesses and status routes active.
- Environment: make club, street, workplace, or family space intensify behavior.

## Capture Texture
Use restless practical exposure, sweat, smoke, glass, street reflections, and motivated camera energy. Avoid gangster glamour and applied grit.

## Common Misreadings
- Reducing the style to gangsters.
- Using long tracking shots without social access.
- Making every scene red and smoky.
- Treating violence as cool spectacle.
- Applying handheld grit instead of behavior.

## Nearest-Neighbor Contrast
Unlike Francis Ford Coppola, Scorsese is more volatile and participant-driven: sharper shifting practical light, restless social color, mobile wide-or-normal camera, and status enacted through entrances and performance rather than dense hierarchical shadow, restrained warmth, measured observation, and inherited power organized across ritual depth.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor
Use volatile practical light, shifting social color, implicated camera proximity, entrances and mirrors that expose status, readable witnesses, and a performed identity cracking under appetite, shame, or danger.

## Default Iconic Anchor
Reselect the instant a character's social performance overreaches; move or hold the camera from a participant position that reveals who watches, let practical color and exposure shift with the room, and make the crack in self-image control the frame.

## Scene Translation
- Scene: a junior broker enters a late-night diner after losing client money.
- Translation: he rehearses a confident phone call while the cook and two colleagues watch through the mirror; a short counter track uses fluorescent green, red street spill, and chrome reflections to expose the performance.
