# Kiyoshi Kurosawa

## Identity
- Chinese name: 黑泽清
- Region: Japan
- Representative works: *Cure*, *Pulse*, *Tokyo Sonata*
- Best suited for: invisible threat, estrangement, ordinary spaces becoming unsafe.

## Core Dramatic Logic
Choose the moment a familiar room or routine reveals a gap in causality. Threat is often offscreen, delayed, or spatially misplaced; characters notice too little or too late.

## Light and Contrast Fingerprint
- Source hierarchy: use flat daylight, weak fluorescents, corridor spill, bare rooms, and practical night sources.
- Shadow and contrast: combine ordinary readable light with one spatially wrong dark zone, unlit doorway, or depth break.
- Highlights and visibility: windows and pale walls may feel exposed while a distant recess refuses information.
- Reject: horror underlighting, colored monster glow, theatrical fog, or darkness spread evenly for mood.

## Color and Exposure Fingerprint
- Palette and saturation: keep institutional beige, pale green, concrete, faded domestic color, and weak exterior blue restrained.
- Skin and midtones: let skin look drained by ordinary ambient light rather than supernatural grading.
- Blacks, whites, exposure: hold pale whites, flat midtones, and a few dense unreadable blacks that have spatial purpose.
- Reject: blue horror filters, green digital sickness, high-contrast monochrome, or desaturation without a threatening gap.

## Lens and Camera Fingerprint
- Distance and lens: use wide or normal medium distance that preserves room geometry and offscreen possibility.
- Height and position: stand at a doorway, corridor, room edge, street, or office where an exit and a blind zone coexist.
- Movement and focus: lock off or move slowly after the character; keep deep context and let the threat enter without emphasis.
- Witness role: a calm observer unable to warn the character, not a frightened handheld victim; reject jump-scare push-ins.

## Composition and Spatial Fingerprint
- Visual center: place ordinary action beside an empty zone that may become causally active.
- Layers and obstruction: use doors, curtains, corridors, windows, partitions, and walls with uncertain continuity.
- Blocking and geometry: keep bodies separated from exits or from one another by unremarkable architecture.
- Information control: show the room clearly but withhold what makes one area dangerous.

## Blocking and Story Translation
- Moment: choose the instant routine continues after an unexplained sign appears.
- Character action: enter, wait, clean, call, or turn toward a sound without immediate reaction.
- Visual center: split attention between mundane task and spatial absence.
- Blocking: leave a plausible route for threat or disappearance.
- Environment: make ordinary architecture create the causal gap.

## Capture Texture
Use plain exposure, weak institutional color, stable depth, and unshowy surfaces. Horror comes from spatial logic, not texture, fog, or digital decay.

## Common Misreadings
- Darkening the whole frame.
- Adding ghosts visibly.
- Using blue-green horror grading.
- Relying on jump-scare framing.
- Making empty space abstract rather than architectural.

## Nearest-Neighbor Contrast
Unlike David Lynch, Kiyoshi Kurosawa keeps the uncanny spatially plain: flatter ordinary source light, drained institutional color, stable wide observation, and one causal void inside readable architecture rather than saturated dream light, unstable tonal shifts, subjective camera dislocation, and symbolic domestic fragments.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor
Use ordinary flat source light, restrained institutional color, stable wide-or-normal room geometry, calm observation, a mundane action, and one unreadable architectural zone that introduces an invisible causal threat.

## Default Iconic Anchor
Reselect the beat after an unexplained sign but before panic; hold a calm camera where the room and exit are readable, keep the task ordinary, and allow one doorway, corridor, or depth zone to remain dark and causally wrong without horror lighting.

## Scene Translation
- Scene: an office cleaner hears a printer start after shutdown.
- Translation: she keeps wiping a desk while paper emerges in the empty rear room; a locked wide view shows the fluorescent office, open exit, and one unlit copy-room doorway without moving closer.
