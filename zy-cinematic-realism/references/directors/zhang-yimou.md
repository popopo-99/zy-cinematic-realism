# Zhang Yimou

## Identity
- Chinese name: 张艺谋
- Region: China
- Representative works: *Raise the Red Lantern*, *Hero*, *Ju Dou*
- Best suited for: ritual, historical power, chromatic conflict, mass choreography.

## Core Dramatic Logic
Choose a public ritual or private defiance in which bodies, color, and architecture reveal authority. Make a repeated order fracture through one precise action.

## Light and Contrast Fingerprint
- Source hierarchy: use sun, courtyard sky, fire, lanterns, windows, or ceremonial practicals to organize power.
- Shadow and contrast: create bold, legible light masses with deep architectural shadow and hard or controlled transitions.
- Highlights and visibility: give costume, fabric, lacquer, dust, or wet stone decisive highlights while subordinate faces may recede.
- Reject: random colored light, soft prestige backlight, muddy battle darkness, or ornamental beams without ritual cause.

## Color and Exposure Fingerprint
- Palette and saturation: build one dominant chromatic relation from costume, architecture, earth, crops, banners, or practical sources.
- Skin and midtones: let skin answer the dominant material color without cosmetic isolation.
- Blacks, whites, exposure: use dense blacks and protected saturated highlights; keep midtones clean enough for graphic color separation.
- Reject: generic red filter, teal-orange action grade, faded heritage sepia, or color detached from objects and power.

## Lens and Camera Fingerprint
- Distance and lens: use wide or normal views for ceremonial mass, measured medium views for defiance, and long lenses to compress ranks or landscape.
- Height and position: choose frontal, elevated, courtyard, gate, or ground-adjacent positions justified by ritual space.
- Movement and focus: use controlled lateral, axial, or crane movement when collective order changes; preserve choreographic depth.
- Witness role: a ceremonial observer who reveals hierarchy, not a chaotic participant; reject random handheld energy.

## Composition and Spatial Fingerprint
- Visual center: place one human deviation against a dominant color or repeated order.
- Layers and obstruction: use gates, screens, walls, fabric, ranks, and landscape bands as graphic planes.
- Blocking and geometry: choreograph repetition, symmetry, diagonals, and masses, then break them intentionally.
- Information control: reveal hierarchy clearly while hiding private motive behind ritual distance or enclosure.

## Blocking and Story Translation
- Moment: choose the instant a ritual is obeyed, refused, or disrupted.
- Character action: step out, lower an object, turn against a line, or cross a color field.
- Visual center: make the deviation legible against collective order.
- Blocking: organize bodies in repeated ranks, circles, or axial layers.
- Environment: let architecture, weather, or landscape become the authority's visible scale.

## Capture Texture
Use material saturation, cloth weight, dust, stone, lacquer, and weather with clear tonal separation. Avoid digital spectacle and decorative color grading.

## Common Misreadings
- Making every scene red.
- Treating crowds as ornamental patterns.
- Using symmetry without power relations.
- Substituting wuxia spectacle for dramatic logic.
- Applying heritage-film polish to ordinary materials.

## Nearest-Neighbor Contrast
Unlike Akira Kurosawa, Zhang stabilizes forces into ceremonial graphic order: more concentrated color masses, cleaner architectural contrast, controlled frontal or elevated camera, and choreographed repetition rather than irregular weather light, material color, responsive action-axis camera, and groups colliding across turbulent depth.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor
Use dominant object-based color, ceremonial source light, bold architectural shadow, controlled camera geometry, repeated mass blocking, and one precise human deviation that fractures visual order.

## Default Iconic Anchor
Reselect the scene as a ritual or collective order breaking; place a measured camera on the governing axis, build one dominant material color against deep architectural shadow, arrange repeated bodies or objects, and make one deviation control the frame.

## Scene Translation
- Scene: workers wait to sign a factory closure notice.
- Translation: blue uniforms form repeated lines beneath a red wall banner while one woman lowers her pen; a frontal courtyard witness uses winter sun and deep gate shadow to turn the refusal into a break in order.
