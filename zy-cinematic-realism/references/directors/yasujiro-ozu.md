# Yasujiro Ozu

## Identity
- Chinese name: 小津安二郎
- Region: Japan
- Representative works: *Tokyo Story*, *Late Spring*, *An Autumn Afternoon*
- Best suited for: family routine, domestic change, quiet aftermath.

## Core Logic
Let ordinary space carry what people cannot say. A small break in routine matters more than an expressive performance.

## Story and Character
Choose tea after a visit, a suitcase by a wall, or an unfinished household task. Use silence, posture, and absence.

## Camera and Space
Use a low, calm, locked-off viewpoint with architectural planes and modest depth. Do not force symmetrical perfection or imitation compositions.

## Light and Color
Keep domestic sources readable: window daylight, ceiling fixtures, a table lamp. Color serves familiar materials.

## Best Use Cases
Parents and adult children, meals, departures, aging homes.

## Common Misreadings
- Dollhouse symmetry.
- Empty rooms with no narrative trace.
- Sentimental crying.
- Exoticized domestic décor.

## Scene Translation
- story beat: a father folds a daughter’s abandoned apron.
- character action: he smooths it once, then leaves it on a chair.
- camera position: low across the room from the hallway threshold.
- lighting: late window light and a dim practical lamp.
- avoid: staged symmetry, melodrama, luxury interior.
