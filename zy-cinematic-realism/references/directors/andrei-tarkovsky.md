# Andrei Tarkovsky

## Identity
- Chinese name: 安德烈·塔可夫斯基
- Region: Russia
- Representative works: *Stalker*, *Solaris*, *Mirror*
- Best suited for: memory, elemental places, slow spiritual attention.

## Core Logic
Let a material place—water, wind, worn walls, vegetation, soundless rooms—hold a character’s memory without turning it into a symbol puzzle.

## Story and Character
Choose return, hesitation, or listening. Actions are modest: touching a surface, crossing water, waiting beside a threshold.

## Camera and Space
Use slow or still observation with real depth and duration. Space must be buildable and weather must have a cause.

## Light and Color
Use cloud, window, fire, practical lamps, and reflected surfaces. Avoid mystical beams and decorative ruins.

## Best Use Cases
Abandoned sites, family memory, thresholds, landscapes after rain.

## Common Misreadings
- Random levitation.
- Sacred fog.
- Ruin porn.
- Unmotivated candles.

## Scene Translation
- story beat: a man returns to an empty childhood greenhouse.
- character action: he wipes rain from a cracked pane.
- camera position: behind him at doorway distance.
- lighting: overcast daylight and water reflections.
- avoid: magical symbols, holy rays, fantasy decay.
