# Andrei Tarkovsky

## Identity
- Chinese name: 安德烈·塔可夫斯基
- Region: Russia
- Representative works: *Stalker*, *Solaris*, *Mirror*
- Best suited for: memory, elemental places, slow spiritual attention.

## Core Logic
Let a material place—water, wind, worn walls, vegetation, soundless rooms—hold a character’s memory without turning it into a symbol puzzle.

## Story and Character
Choose return, hesitation, or listening. Actions are modest: touching a surface, crossing water, waiting beside a threshold.

## Camera and Space
Use slow or still observation with real depth and duration. Space must be buildable and weather must have a cause.

## Light and Color
Use cloud, window, fire, practical lamps, and reflected surfaces. Avoid mystical beams and decorative ruins.

## Best Use Cases
Abandoned sites, family memory, thresholds, landscapes after rain.

## Common Misreadings
- Random levitation.
- Sacred fog.
- Ruin porn.
- Unmotivated candles.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor

- Explicit director token: Andrei Tarkovsky
- Representative film anchors: *Stalker*, *Mirror*, *Solaris*
- Strong visual grammar: patient normal or wide-lens immersion in deep tactile space; bodies crossing unresolved thresholds; slow internal change rather than plot punctuation; water, air, fire, surfaces, and material decay act on the scene; dim practical or natural light with complex tonal transitions; earthbound, restrained color; deep or breathing focus; organic capture texture; memory and duration treated as physical dramatic matter.
- Do not reduce to: random puddles, floating objects, symbolic ruins, religious props, or decorative decay.
- Prompt insertion: Director reference: Andrei Tarkovsky, with broad visual reference points in *Stalker*, *Mirror*, and *Solaris*. Treat duration, material space, water, air, fire, surfaces, and memory as active dramatic matter; use slow internal change, layered sound-like visual rhythm, unresolved thresholds, deep tactile environments, and spiritually charged but physically grounded imagery. Avoid arbitrary puddles, floating objects, or symbolic ruin decoration.
