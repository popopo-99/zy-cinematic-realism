# Andrei Tarkovsky

## Identity
- Chinese name: 安德烈·塔可夫斯基
- Region: Russia
- Representative works: *Stalker*, *Mirror*, *Solaris*
- Best suited for: memory, elemental places, thresholds, slow spiritual attention.

## Core Dramatic Logic
Choose return, hesitation, or listening inside a material place. Water, air, fire, vegetation, worn rooms, and duration carry memory without becoming a symbol puzzle.

## Light and Contrast Fingerprint
- Source hierarchy: use overcast sky, windows, fire, practical lamps, and reflected water as the only important sources.
- Shadow and contrast: build complex low-to-moderate tonal transitions, with deep unresolved recesses and light changing across duration.
- Highlights and visibility: water, glass, metal, or flame may hold unstable highlights while a face remains partly absorbed by the room.
- Reject: sacred beams, decorative candles, fantasy glow, or mystical fog without weather and source logic.

## Color and Exposure Fingerprint
- Palette and saturation: favor earth, oxidized metal, wet vegetation, old paint, and restrained source color; shifts may follow memory or material state.
- Skin and midtones: let skin inherit damp air, window coolness, fire warmth, or practical dimness.
- Blacks, whites, exposure: permit dense blacks, breathing midtones, reflected whites, and gradual exposure drift through space.
- Reject: generic sepia spirituality, green ruin filters, monochrome symbolism, or polished desaturation.

## Lens and Camera Fingerprint
- Distance and lens: use normal or wide-lens immersion that allows duration and deep tactile space.
- Height and position: occupy a physically traversable doorway, path, room edge, or waterline at patient human height.
- Movement and focus: hold still or move slowly with spatial purpose; use deep or breathing focus rather than synthetic bokeh.
- Witness role: a contemplative participant moving through matter, not an omniscient symbol-maker; reject arbitrary floating camera and montage shorthand.

## Composition and Spatial Fingerprint
- Visual center: allow a threshold, surface, or slow bodily action to compete with the person.
- Layers and obstruction: use water, glass, doors, vegetation, walls, and air as material layers.
- Blocking and geometry: make bodies cross, pause, or listen within unresolved depth rather than pose before picturesque decay.
- Information control: hide causal explanation while keeping the physical route and material consequence believable.

## Blocking and Story Translation
- Moment: choose the interval before a return is accepted or after memory interrupts a task.
- Character action: touch, cross, listen, wipe, wait, or carry something through a threshold.
- Visual center: privilege material change and duration over facial declaration.
- Blocking: let the body negotiate a real obstacle or layered route.
- Environment: make water, wind, fire, decay, or vegetation alter pace.

## Capture Texture
Use organic softness, wet and oxidized surfaces, breathing focus, slow exposure transitions, and unpolished material density. Do not decorate the frame with ruin, grain, or transcendence.

## Common Misreadings
- Adding random puddles or floating objects.
- Using fog as instant spirituality.
- Beautifying abandoned places as ruin porn.
- Filling rooms with unmotivated candles.
- Replacing duration with symbolic props.

## Nearest-Neighbor Contrast
Unlike Apichatpong Weerasethakul, Tarkovsky drives memory through weighty elemental transformation: denser tonal transitions, earthbound restrained color, patient traversing camera, and material thresholds pressing on the body rather than porous tropical daylight, casual stillness, and unseen presences coexisting with ordinary life.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor
Use patient normal-or-wide immersion, unresolved material thresholds, water/air/fire/surfaces acting on duration, dim motivated tonal transitions, earthbound color, breathing focus, and memory embodied by a modest physical action.

## Default Iconic Anchor
Reselect the scene for slow internal change at a real threshold; place a patient camera inside deep tactile space, let elemental material alter a small action, preserve dense recesses and restrained source color, and keep memory physical without mystical beams or symbolic ruin.

## Scene Translation
- Scene: a man returns to an empty childhood greenhouse.
- Translation: he wipes rain from a cracked pane and stops at a blocked inner door; a patient doorway view holds wet glass, deep plants, overcast reflection, and his incomplete passage in breathing focus.
