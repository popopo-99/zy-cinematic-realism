# Alfonso Cuarón

## Identity
- Chinese name: 阿方索·卡隆
- Region: Mexico
- Representative works: *Y Tu Mamá También*, *Children of Men*, *Roma*
- Best suited for: social context, continuous action, domestic labor, crisis moving through space.

## Core Dramatic Logic
Choose an action whose meaning changes as the camera and characters move through a larger social environment. Private life and public consequence remain visible in the same spatial continuum.

## Light and Contrast Fingerprint
- Source hierarchy: use daylight, windows, street sources, vehicle light, household practicals, fire, weather, and emergency sources encountered along the route.
- Shadow and contrast: let contrast evolve naturally across rooms, streets, and weather while maintaining spatial continuity.
- Highlights and visibility: water, windows, vehicles, skin, smoke from real events, and bright exteriors change exposure as the camera travels.
- Reject: uniform prestige light, decorative backlight, action haze, or impossible source continuity.

## Color and Exposure Fingerprint
- Palette and saturation: preserve location-specific streets, skin, concrete, water, domestic materials, and weather; monochrome is not a default.
- Skin and midtones: let skin adapt realistically as people cross source zones.
- Blacks, whites, exposure: accept exposure adjustment, bright exteriors, deep interiors, and dynamic midtones without HDR flattening.
- Reject: teal-orange crisis grading, nostalgic Mexico color, grey dystopian wash, or polished monochrome imitation.

## Lens and Camera Fingerprint
- Distance and lens: favor wide or normal spatial immersion that keeps bodies and environment in causal relation.
- Height and position: move at walking, vehicle, room, street, shore, or stair height along a physically continuous route.
- Movement and focus: use extended tracks, pans, or vehicle-mounted movement when the evolving environment changes meaning; maintain adaptive contextual focus.
- Witness role: a mobile social participant, not a weightless spectacle camera; reject impossible threading through solid space.

## Composition and Spatial Fingerprint
- Visual center: allow attention to migrate from private action to public consequence and back.
- Layers and obstruction: use traffic, rooms, windows, crowds, vehicles, water, and architecture as changing contextual layers.
- Blocking and geometry: choreograph entries, exits, and offscreen events along a continuous route.
- Information control: reveal background social facts before characters fully register them.

## Blocking and Story Translation
- Moment: choose a private task interrupted by public movement or crisis.
- Character action: clean, drive, carry, escort, search, or protect while continuing through space.
- Visual center: transfer attention between person and environment.
- Blocking: coordinate background events with a real route.
- Environment: make streets, house, vehicle, or weather change the task.

## Capture Texture
Use embodied wide-camera movement, natural exposure adaptation, location sound-like visual density, water, dust, and ordinary surfaces. Avoid digital one-take smoothness.

## Common Misreadings
- Treating any long take as Cuarón-like.
- Using impossible camera paths.
- Hiding social context in shallow focus.
- Applying grey dystopian color.
- Turning crisis into technical spectacle.

## Nearest-Neighbor Contrast
Unlike Alejandro G. Iñárritu-style intensity, Cuarón keeps movement geographically and socially explanatory: more natural source adaptation, location-specific color, wide contextual camera, and attention migrating to background systems rather than subjective exposure extremes, visceral close pursuit, and emotional ordeal dominating the environment.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor
Use a physically continuous wide-or-normal camera route, evolving motivated light, location-specific color, adaptive focus, private action sharing space with public consequence, and background events that change meaning without technical spectacle.

## Default Iconic Anchor
Reselect a private task as it crosses into public consequence; design one walkable or drivable camera route, let exposure and focus adapt to real sources, and transfer visual authority between character and surrounding social action.

## Scene Translation
- Scene: a housekeeper carries laundry home during a neighborhood evacuation.
- Translation: the camera follows from courtyard through gate to street as children pass, a bus reverses, and smoke appears beyond roofs; window shade gives way to hard afternoon sun without cutting geography.
