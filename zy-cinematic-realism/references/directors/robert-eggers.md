# Robert Eggers

## Identity
- Chinese name: 罗伯特·艾格斯
- Region: United States
- Representative works: *The Witch*, *The Lighthouse*, *The Northman*
- Best suited for: historical belief, elemental dread, isolation, ritual and labor.

## Core Dramatic Logic
Choose a task or ritual whose period belief system makes danger materially real to the characters. Weather, labor, language, and architecture trap bodies before the uncanny is confirmed.

## Light and Contrast Fingerprint
- Source hierarchy: use overcast sky, fire, candle, hearth, oil lamp, window, moon or snow reflection only when historically and physically credible.
- Shadow and contrast: accept severe low-light falloff, deep corners, hard fire transitions, and pale exterior fields.
- Highlights and visibility: flame, wet skin, wool, wood, salt, metal, and weather catch limited harsh highlights.
- Reject: modern fill light, blue moonlight presets, fantasy beams, glossy candlelight, or readable darkness without a source.

## Color and Exposure Fingerprint
- Palette and saturation: restrict color to period cloth, timber, earth, soot, sea, skin, fire, and weather.
- Skin and midtones: let skin appear cold, smoke-stained, fire-warmed, wet, or underexposed according to labor and source.
- Blacks, whites, exposure: permit dense blacks, chalky skies, candle clipping, and narrow midtone range without digital murk.
- Reject: prestige sepia, teal-orange history, desaturated horror filter, or jewel color unsupported by materials.

## Lens and Camera Fingerprint
- Distance and lens: use severe wide or normal observation for architecture and labor, with controlled closer views for ritual detail.
- Height and position: stand at hut, field, shore, table, deck, forest edge, or ritual circle where period space remains buildable.
- Movement and focus: lock, pan, or move slowly with labor and ritual; preserve physical depth and avoid weightless supernatural motion.
- Witness role: a stern historical observer inside the belief system, not a modern skeptical tourist; reject flashy horror coverage.

## Composition and Spatial Fingerprint
- Visual center: place a body or ritual object inside oppressive elemental or architectural order.
- Layers and obstruction: use beams, doors, smoke from real fire, trees, sea, weather, tools, and animals.
- Blocking and geometry: arrange labor and ritual in severe lines, clusters, or circles whose logic belongs to the period.
- Information control: show material hardship and belief clearly while withholding whether the threat is supernatural.

## Blocking and Story Translation
- Moment: choose labor or ritual immediately before belief becomes action.
- Character action: tend, bind, cut, pray, haul, listen, or refuse.
- Visual center: connect body, tool, and elemental pressure.
- Blocking: keep community order or isolation physically severe.
- Environment: make weather, fire, sea, forest, or architecture constrain survival.

## Capture Texture
Use historically credible low light, rough wool, timber, soot, wet skin, weather, and grounded optical severity. Avoid prestige antiquing and horror polish.

## Common Misreadings
- Making every scene monochrome.
- Adding blue moonlight.
- Using candles beyond plausible brightness.
- Treating dialect, costume, or dirt as the style.
- Revealing the supernatural too clearly.

## Nearest-Neighbor Contrast
Unlike David Lynch, Eggers keeps dread historically and materially constrained: source-poor elemental light, restricted earthbound color, severe stable wide-or-normal camera, and ritual bodies trapped by belief rather than theatrical practical pools, saturated psychological color, creeping subjective camera, and identity rupturing across domestic dream seams.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor
Use historically credible source-poor light, restricted material color, severe wide-or-normal observation, elemental pressure, period labor or ritual, dense falloff, and belief becoming action before the uncanny is confirmed.

## Default Iconic Anchor
Reselect the task or ritual just before belief dictates danger; place a stern camera inside a buildable period space, limit illumination to credible sources, let body, tool, and weather form the composition, and keep the supernatural unconfirmed.

## Scene Translation
- Scene: lighthouse keepers discover the supply boat has not arrived.
- Translation: one counts the final oil tins while the other bars the storm door; a severe wide interior uses one lamp, white window surf, black rafters, wet wool, and the staircase as an unresolved route.
