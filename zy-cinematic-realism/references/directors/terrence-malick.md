# Terrence Malick

## Identity
- Chinese name: 泰伦斯·马力克
- Region: United States
- Representative works: *Days of Heaven*, *The Thin Red Line*, *The Tree of Life*
- Best suited for: natural light, tactile attention, fragmented memory.

## Core Logic
Let lived sensation lead: grass underfoot, wind against fabric, a hand on worn wood, changing light across a face. Keep it connected to a real action.

## Story and Character
Choose an interval between decisions, especially outdoors or near a threshold. Characters can move, touch, or notice rather than pose.

## Camera and Space
Use a close, drifting or observational position only where a human camera could travel. Balance intimate detail with surrounding life.

## Light and Color
Favor real sun, cloud, dusk, window light, and reflected surfaces. Do not invent golden-hour light at the wrong time.

## Best Use Cases
Rural work, childhood memory, family passage, landscape and private reflection.

## Common Misreadings
- Perpetual sun flare.
- Abstract whispery montage.
- Backlit fashion portrait.
- Random nature inserts.

## Scene Translation
- story beat: a woman pauses during orchard work after receiving news.
- character action: she rubs soil from her palm before returning to the basket.
- camera position: nearby at waist height, moving with her path.
- lighting: late cloud-filtered daylight through leaves.
- avoid: perfume-ad light, excessive flare, pastoral fantasy.
