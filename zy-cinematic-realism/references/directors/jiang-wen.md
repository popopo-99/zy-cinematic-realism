# Jiang Wen

## Identity
- Chinese name: 姜文
- Region: China
- Representative works: *In the Heat of the Sun*, *Let the Bullets Fly*, *Devils on the Doorstep*
- Best suited for: volatile power games, masculine bravado, historical absurdity, unreliable memory.

## Core Dramatic Logic
Choose the instant confidence becomes performance and performance exposes danger. Let competing claims of authority collide through bodies, objects, and sharp tonal reversals.

## Light and Contrast Fingerprint
- Source hierarchy: use forceful sun, hard window light, fire, bare bulbs, and practical interiors with clear directional authority.
- Shadow and contrast: favor bold hard contrast, abrupt tonal shifts, and hot or dark zones that make power unstable.
- Highlights and visibility: sweat, dust, metal, food, and faces can flare under heat while corners hold suspicion.
- Reject: soft tasteful prestige light, even period illumination, heroic rim light, or atmospheric murk.

## Color and Exposure Fingerprint
- Palette and saturation: use sun-baked earth, military or civic materials, hot skin, flags, painted walls, and food as forceful but grounded color.
- Skin and midtones: allow heat, redness, dust, and source contamination to remain physical.
- Blacks, whites, exposure: tolerate hot highlights, firm blacks, and punchy midtones without commercial gloss.
- Reject: heritage sepia, uniform patriotic red, teal-orange action grading, or nostalgic softness.

## Lens and Camera Fingerprint
- Distance and lens: use assertive wide or normal proximity for confrontation, with longer lenses for suspicious counter-observation.
- Height and position: occupy the table edge, courtyard, vehicle, or crowd at a bold but physically plausible height.
- Movement and focus: pan, surge, or hold abruptly as authority shifts; maintain enough depth for competing reactions.
- Witness role: an energized participant who distrusts the performance, not a neutral historian; reject weightless spectacle moves.

## Composition and Spatial Fingerprint
- Visual center: make a boast, object, or command compete with contradictory reactions.
- Layers and obstruction: use tables, weapons, food, doors, crowds, smoke from real activity, and architecture as pressure.
- Blocking and geometry: arrange power triangles, sudden reversals, and bodies claiming or losing territory.
- Information control: reveal performance clearly while leaving the true balance of power unstable.

## Blocking and Story Translation
- Moment: choose the instant a confident statement becomes dangerous or absurd.
- Character action: raise, slam, toast, point, sit, or refuse.
- Visual center: split attention between the performance and a dissenting reaction.
- Blocking: create a triangle of authority rather than a frontal hero.
- Environment: let heat, crowd, table, vehicle, or courtyard amplify conflict.

## Capture Texture
Use hard sun, sweat, dust, rough practical interiors, and kinetic but readable bodies. Avoid retro polish, patriotic spectacle, and comic-book exaggeration.

## Common Misreadings
- Treating swagger as uncomplicated heroism.
- Making every scene loud or comic.
- Applying sepia to history.
- Using low angles without a power reversal.
- Replacing physical heat with saturated grading.

## Nearest-Neighbor Contrast
Unlike Zhang Yimou, Jiang Wen destabilizes order from inside performance: harder volatile light, hotter mixed material color, assertive participant camera, and triangular improvised blocking rather than ceremonial source hierarchy, concentrated color masses, measured axes, and repeated graphic choreography.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor
Use volatile hard source light, hot material color, assertive wide-or-normal proximity, competing reactions, power triangles, and a boast or command that tips from confidence into absurd danger.

## Default Iconic Anchor
Reselect the instant a performance of authority begins to fail; put a bold physical camera close to the contested space, keep at least two contradictory reactions visible, use hard heat-driven light and punchy material color, and let blocking reveal the reversal.

## Scene Translation
- Scene: a village committee announces a compulsory relocation.
- Translation: the official raises a stamped notice while a farmer calmly moves his chair into the aisle; a wide table-edge witness keeps laughter, alarm, and the blocked exit active under hard noon window light.
