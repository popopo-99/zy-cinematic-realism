# Bi Gan

## Identity
- Chinese name: 毕赣
- Region: China
- Representative works: *Kaili Blues*, *Long Day's Journey into Night*, *Resurrection*
- Best suited for: memory labyrinths, humid towns, subjective journeys, temporal slippage.

## Core Dramatic Logic
Choose a search or return in which present action and memory occupy the same traversable place. Let routes, sounds, objects, and weather fold time without abandoning physical continuity.

## Light and Contrast Fingerprint
- Source hierarchy: use humid daylight, bare bulbs, shop fluorescents, vehicle lamps, projection, or sparse night practicals.
- Shadow and contrast: allow long passages of low, mixed contrast punctuated by local practical pools and deep unreadable turns.
- Highlights and visibility: wet surfaces, mirrors, bulbs, and windows may bloom as the camera crosses them.
- Reject: fantasy glow, uniform neon, mystical shafts, or lighting changes without a traversed source.

## Color and Exposure Fingerprint
- Palette and saturation: build humid green, rust, blue, tungsten, or painted-wall relationships from location and practical sources.
- Skin and midtones: let skin drift through source contamination and partial underexposure during movement.
- Blacks, whites, exposure: tolerate blooming bulbs, dense alley blacks, exposure adaptation, and imperfect midtone continuity.
- Reject: teal-magenta dream grading, sepia memory filters, pastel surrealism, or polished color continuity.

## Lens and Camera Fingerprint
- Distance and lens: use wide-to-normal immersive proximity that can hold bodies and route; occasional longer observation marks inaccessible memory.
- Height and position: travel at walking, riding, or room height along a physically continuous path.
- Movement and focus: favor patient tracking, circling, or following with focus adaptation and temporal duration; every move must have a walkable route.
- Witness role: a searching consciousness inside space, not a drone or music-video camera; reject impossible transitions disguised as atmosphere.

## Composition and Spatial Fingerprint
- Visual center: transfer attention from person to object to route without cutting spatial logic.
- Layers and obstruction: use doorways, tunnels, mirrors, vehicles, rain, and nested rooms as temporal thresholds.
- Blocking and geometry: characters appear, disappear, or exchange positions along a continuous path.
- Information control: reveal the route but withhold whether an encounter belongs to memory, dream, or present.

## Blocking and Story Translation
- Moment: choose a search that crosses an ordinary threshold into remembered duration.
- Character action: walk, ride, follow a sound, exchange an object, or return to a room.
- Visual center: let a recurring object hand attention to the next space.
- Blocking: choreograph entries and exits along a continuous path.
- Environment: make humidity, night traffic, corridors, or sound-implied space guide movement.

## Capture Texture
Use humid air, practical bloom, imperfect focus adaptation, wet walls, and long embodied movement. Avoid glossy surrealism and generic dream haze.

## Common Misreadings
- Calling any long take Bi Gan-like.
- Adding green neon without source.
- Using dream logic that breaks physical space.
- Filling scenes with random symbolic objects.
- Polishing humidity into music-video gloss.

## Nearest-Neighbor Contrast
Unlike Andrei Tarkovsky, Bi Gan makes memory mobile and route-driven: mixed practical bloom, humid local color, walking wide-camera continuity, and nested urban thresholds rather than weighty elemental light, earthbound restraint, patient fixed immersion, and matter slowing the body at unresolved spiritual boundaries.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor
Use a physically continuous search through humid layered space, source-bound practical bloom, color contamination, immersive wide-to-normal movement, focus adaptation, recurring objects, and temporal uncertainty held inside a walkable route.

## Default Iconic Anchor
Reselect the scene as a search crossing present and memory; give the camera one continuous human route, let a recurring object transfer the visual center through nested thresholds, and accept practical bloom, exposure adaptation, humid color, and incomplete temporal certainty.

## Scene Translation
- Scene: a man searches a closed arcade for a cassette left by his brother.
- Translation: the camera follows him from rain-dark street through a half-lit game hall as the cassette changes hands at a stairwell; green fluorescents, tungsten bulbs, and wet glass shift exposure along one walkable path.
