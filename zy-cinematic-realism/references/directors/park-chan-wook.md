# Park Chan-wook

## Identity
- Chinese name: 朴赞郁
- Region: South Korea
- Representative works: *Oldboy*, *The Handmaiden*, *Decision to Leave*
- Best suited for: desire, revenge, deception, psychologically charged objects and spaces.

## Core Dramatic Logic
Choose an action in which desire, control, and concealment share one object or spatial relation. Visual elegance should sharpen moral discomfort, not replace it.

## Light and Contrast Fingerprint
- Source hierarchy: use windows, lamps, candles, screens, exterior weather, or institutional practicals with carefully controlled direction.
- Shadow and contrast: build sculpted local contrast, reflected light, and sharp transitions that bind faces to objects or architecture.
- Highlights and visibility: skin, fabric, metal, glass, wood, or water may receive sensual highlights while motive remains shadowed.
- Reject: random colored glamour, glossy rim light, universal low-key mood, or beauty illumination without psychological function.

## Color and Exposure Fingerprint
- Palette and saturation: use controlled jewel, earth, green, blue, red, or neutral relations tied to costume, décor, landscape, and source light.
- Skin and midtones: let skin respond precisely to nearby material and reflected color.
- Blacks, whites, exposure: maintain rich blacks, protected whites, and shaped midtones with selective saturation.
- Reject: arbitrary teal-orange, neon revenge grading, oversaturated luxury, or color motifs disconnected from desire and control.

## Lens and Camera Fingerprint
- Distance and lens: alternate precise close object-body relations with wider architectural reveals; lens choice may create elegant unease.
- Height and position: use drawers, mirrors, stairs, ceilings, corridors, tables, or landscape lines as physically motivated viewpoints.
- Movement and focus: employ controlled tracks, pivots, overheads, or focus transfers when they reveal concealed relation.
- Witness role: an exact conspiratorial observer, not a chaotic victim; reject movement that exists only for virtuosity.

## Composition and Spatial Fingerprint
- Visual center: bind a charged object to a hand, face, or hidden observer.
- Layers and obstruction: use mirrors, screens, doors, fabric, furniture, stairs, and landscape folds to double or conceal relationships.
- Blocking and geometry: choreograph bodies through precise lines, reversals, and visual rhymes.
- Information control: reveal one layer elegantly while withholding the power relation behind it.

## Blocking and Story Translation
- Moment: choose the instant an intimate gesture becomes evidence or control.
- Character action: fold, exchange, observe, hide, fasten, wash, or touch an object.
- Visual center: connect the object to two competing desires.
- Blocking: create a precise triangle among actor, object, and observer.
- Environment: make décor, weather, or architecture participate in concealment.

## Capture Texture
Use tactile fabric, wood, skin, metal, water, and controlled optical transitions. Preserve material sensuality without fashion-ad polish.

## Common Misreadings
- Treating elegance as the whole style.
- Using violent imagery without desire or control.
- Applying jewel tones indiscriminately.
- Adding overhead shots without a concealment function.
- Turning sensual surfaces into luxury advertising.

## Nearest-Neighbor Contrast
Unlike Bong Joon-ho, Park compresses social meaning into charged object-body geometry: more sculpted light, controlled saturated palettes, expressive precise camera moves, and triangular concealment rather than functional source light, ordinary social color, wide ensemble geography, and status reversals across levels.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor
Use sculpted motivated light, controlled material color, precise object-body relations, expressive but physical camera geometry, mirrored or layered concealment, and an intimate gesture that becomes evidence of desire or control.

## Default Iconic Anchor
Reselect the instant a beautiful or ordinary gesture changes power; construct a precise triangle among body, object, and observer, shape source light and material color around that relation, and use one controlled camera reveal without luxury polish.

## Scene Translation
- Scene: a translator returns a borrowed fountain pen after a secret meeting.
- Translation: her thumb wipes ink from the clip while the owner watches through a mirror; a slow table-height pivot joins hand, reflected eye, and locked drawer under window rain and one green-shaded lamp.
