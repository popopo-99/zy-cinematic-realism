# King Hu

## Identity
- Chinese name: 胡金铨
- Region: China / Hong Kong / Taiwan
- Representative works: *A Touch of Zen*, *Dragon Inn*, *Come Drink with Me*
- Best suited for: spatially legible action, thresholds, martial suspense, landscape and architecture.

## Core Dramatic Logic
Choose the pause in which fighters read a space before movement erupts. Architecture, terrain, sound-implied offscreen threat, and precise entrances make action intelligible.

## Light and Contrast Fingerprint
- Source hierarchy: use daylight, courtyard sky, windows, lanterns, fire, and weather that belong to inn, temple, forest, or road.
- Shadow and contrast: preserve clear zones for spatial reading, with hard threshold shadows and moving patches from foliage or structure.
- Highlights and visibility: blades, fabric, dust, wood, and faces catch brief directional highlights during movement.
- Reject: fantasy energy glow, universal backlight, murky battle scenes, or modern action-rim lighting.

## Color and Exposure Fingerprint
- Palette and saturation: derive restrained but distinct color from costume rank, timber, earth, vegetation, plaster, and practical fire.
- Skin and midtones: keep skin integrated with daylight or lantern sources, not cosmetically isolated.
- Blacks, whites, exposure: hold readable midtones for action, dense doorway blacks, and protected daylight or flame highlights.
- Reject: wuxia candy color, teal-orange spectacle, sepia antiquity, or monochrome ink-paint imitation.

## Lens and Camera Fingerprint
- Distance and lens: use wide or normal spatial clarity for action geography, with measured closer views for a hand, glance, or concealed weapon.
- Height and position: stand beside a gate, courtyard edge, inn table, roofline, or forest path where routes remain plausible.
- Movement and focus: hold, pan, or cut through motivated directional changes; preserve deep spatial cues and avoid weightless camera acrobatics.
- Witness role: a tactically attentive observer, not a fighter-mounted action camera.

## Composition and Spatial Fingerprint
- Visual center: make a threshold, line of sight, or concealed entrance as important as the combatant.
- Layers and obstruction: use screens, pillars, doors, rooflines, trees, curtains, and furniture to reveal and conceal routes.
- Blocking and geometry: choreograph entrances, pauses, feints, and directional bursts across clear axes and vertical levels.
- Information control: let viewers know the space but not always the hidden opponent's timing.

## Blocking and Story Translation
- Moment: choose the listening pause before an ambush or the instant a route is recognized.
- Character action: stop, listen, test, conceal, or cross a threshold.
- Visual center: privilege spatial intelligence over impact.
- Blocking: establish multiple possible entries and one decisive line.
- Environment: make architecture or terrain determine tactics.

## Capture Texture
Use physical fabric, dust, timber, foliage, grounded momentum, and readable motion. Avoid wire-fantasy weightlessness and digital action polish.

## Common Misreadings
- Reducing the method to swordplay.
- Making movement weightless.
- Cutting too fast to read geography.
- Styling historical spaces as museum sets.
- Using smoke and backlight as wuxia shorthand.

## Nearest-Neighbor Contrast
Unlike Zhang Yimou, King Hu prioritizes tactical thresholds over mass spectacle: more even readable action light, restrained material color, grounded wide camera geography, and entrances across layered architecture rather than dominant chromatic masses, ceremonial contrast, elevated axes, and repeated collective choreography.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor
Use a listening pause, readable wide-or-normal action geography, source-based threshold light, restrained costume and material color, layered architecture, concealed entrances, and precise directional blocking before physical release.

## Default Iconic Anchor
Reselect the beat before action, when a character reads the space; place a grounded camera where all viable routes can be understood, use doors, pillars, terrain, and shadows to conceal timing, and let one decisive entrance break the still geometry.

## Scene Translation
- Scene: a courier suspects an ambush inside a roadside repair shop.
- Translation: she stops with a parcel halfway across the doorway as a hanging chain moves in the rear bay; a wide counter-edge view maps two exits, a loft, and the sunlit yard under practical shop shadow.
