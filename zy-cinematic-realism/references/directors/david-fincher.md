# David Fincher

## Identity
- Chinese name: 大卫·芬奇
- Region: United States
- Representative works: *Se7en*, *Zodiac*, *The Social Network*
- Best suited for: investigation, procedure, controlled interiors, uneasy detail.

## Core Logic
Make observation methodical. Tension comes from incomplete evidence, repeated process, and a camera that notices what a character misses.

## Story and Character
Choose review, searching, recording, or a decision delayed by detail. Emotion appears in concentration and fatigue, not aggression.

## Camera and Space
Use precise but physically plausible placement; desks, doorframes, files, lists, and work surfaces can organize the frame. The camera must have a stated observation purpose.

## Light and Color
Use available office, street, task, or screen-adjacent light with readable exposure. Avoid a uniform green filter.

## Best Use Cases
Investigations, interviews, online work, institutional routines, late-night research.

## Common Misreadings
- Constant darkness.
- Grimy clutter everywhere.
- Aggressive color cast.
- Music-video camera moves.
- Turning an ordinary room into a crime scene.

## Signature Overrides

When strength is `strong` or `iconic`, enforce the following differences:

- Story beat bias: choose a verification, correction, comparison, or incomplete procedure.
- Blocking pattern: place people and props so cause, evidence, and response can be read across the room.
- Camera height: select one exact, physically plausible height for a clear observation purpose.
- Camera distance / lens behavior: measured medium or compressed observation; do not use arbitrary intimacy.
- Movement grammar: locked-off or minimally adjusted only to reveal a needed information relationship.
- Light and contrast: low illumination is permitted only when the necessary faces, paper, hands, or objects remain readable.
- Color behavior: neutral, restrained material color; no default yellow-green cast.
- Depth-of-field strategy: control focus around information nodes while retaining enough context to understand the procedure.
- Texture / capture character: clean but not glossy, with matte surfaces and controlled shadow detail rather than digital crispness.
- Environment participation: doors, tables, lists, keys, boxes, and worn surfaces become causal information, never forensic decoration.
