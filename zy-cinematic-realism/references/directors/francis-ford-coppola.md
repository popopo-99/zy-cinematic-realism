# Francis Ford Coppola

## Identity
- Chinese name: 弗朗西斯·福特·科波拉
- Region: United States
- Representative works: *The Godfather*, *The Conversation*, *Apocalypse Now*
- Best suited for: family power, secrecy, moral compromise, institutions becoming mythic.

## Core Dramatic Logic
Choose a private decision whose consequences extend through family, organization, or state power. Silence, listening, ritual, and withheld reaction make authority feel inherited and corrosive.

## Light and Contrast Fingerprint
- Source hierarchy: use windows, shaded lamps, practical interiors, exterior heat, fire, or public-event light with strong motivated direction.
- Shadow and contrast: allow faces to emerge from dense rooms, broad top shadow, or layered practical pools while power centers remain legible.
- Highlights and visibility: polished wood, smoke from real activity, uniforms, sweat, glass, and eyes catch restrained highlights.
- Reject: generic gangster darkness, universal top light, glossy sepia, or theatrical shadow without power logic.

## Color and Exposure Fingerprint
- Palette and saturation: use warm interiors, dark wood, skin, olive, black clothing, exterior heat, or surveillance neutrals according to setting.
- Skin and midtones: let skin become warm, sallow, or dim through actual room sources and moral atmosphere.
- Blacks, whites, exposure: hold deep blacks, protected lamps and windows, and rich midtones without crushing every face.
- Reject: blanket amber Godfather grade, teal-orange war color, vintage brown wash, or luxury polish.

## Lens and Camera Fingerprint
- Distance and lens: favor measured medium or longer-lens observation for power and listening; use wides when institutional or landscape scale expands consequence.
- Height and position: occupy table edges, doorways, listening posts, crowds, boats, or rooms where alliances can be read.
- Movement and focus: use patient pushes, pans, or restrained tracks when moral alignment changes; preserve layered reactions.
- Witness role: a grave participant listening for consequence, not a frenetic street observer; reject showy movement.

## Composition and Spatial Fingerprint
- Visual center: link a quiet decision to a family or institutional hierarchy.
- Layers and obstruction: use doorways, tables, curtains, crowds, recording equipment, and depth to conceal allegiance.
- Blocking and geometry: place authority centrally or deeply while petitioners and witnesses occupy subordinate planes.
- Information control: reveal who controls the room while withholding the private cost of that control.

## Blocking and Story Translation
- Moment: choose assent, refusal, or listening before irreversible consequence.
- Character action: receive, close a door, lower a voice, listen, sign, or remain seated.
- Visual center: privilege the transfer of authority.
- Blocking: use layered hierarchy and withheld reaction.
- Environment: let family ritual, office protocol, or public event carry institutional weight.

## Capture Texture
Use rich but source-bound darkness, tactile wood, fabric, heat, smoke, and patient focus. Avoid gangster pastiche and prestige sepia.

## Common Misreadings
- Applying amber darkness to every interior.
- Treating organized crime as the only subject.
- Using solemnity without moral consequence.
- Copying famous compositions.
- Crushing faces into unreadable black.

## Nearest-Neighbor Contrast
Unlike Martin Scorsese, Coppola is slower and more hierarchical: denser source-bound shadow, richer restrained warmth, measured medium or long observation, and layered ritual power rather than nervous practical contrast, volatile street color, mobile participant camera, and behavior erupting through social performance.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor
Use dense motivated interior shadow, restrained rich material color, measured observation, layered family or institutional hierarchy, patient reaction, and a quiet decision whose consequences extend beyond the room.

## Default Iconic Anchor
Reselect the beat when authority silently changes hands; place a measured camera where petition, witness, and power occupy distinct depths, let practical pools and dense shadow conceal cost, and keep the gesture quieter than its consequence.

## Scene Translation
- Scene: a family business decides whether to dismiss a longtime driver.
- Translation: the eldest sibling folds the driver's keys into a cloth while the father remains behind a half-closed study door; a measured dining-room view uses shaded lamps and window dusk to layer petition, decision, and inherited authority.
