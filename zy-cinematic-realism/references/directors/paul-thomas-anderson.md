# Paul Thomas Anderson

## Identity
- Chinese name: 保罗·托马斯·安德森
- Region: United States
- Representative works: *There Will Be Blood*, *The Master*, *Phantom Thread*
- Best suited for: power duels, obsessive work, family substitutes, volatile intimacy.

## Core Dramatic Logic
Choose a social or work ritual in which two people negotiate dependence and control. Performance accumulates through gesture, language, and spatial encroachment before a rupture.

## Light and Contrast Fingerprint
- Source hierarchy: use daylight, windows, oil lamps, work lamps, dining practicals, stage or institutional sources appropriate to era.
- Shadow and contrast: favor rich naturalistic contrast with faces moving between broad source fields and intimate practical pools.
- Highlights and visibility: skin, fabric, oil, wood, glass, food, and machinery carry tactile highlights without glamour.
- Reject: prestige amber wash, universal backlight, polished period glow, or expressionistic shadow without a power exchange.

## Color and Exposure Fingerprint
- Palette and saturation: derive restrained rich color from period materials, skin, work clothes, interiors, landscape, and food.
- Skin and midtones: keep skin textured and responsive to source warmth, fatigue, sweat, or illness.
- Blacks, whites, exposure: preserve deep material blacks, creamy or daylight whites, and substantial midtones.
- Reject: sepia prestige grading, teal-orange period color, pastel romance, or luxurious fabric color without labor.

## Lens and Camera Fingerprint
- Distance and lens: use medium-wide ensemble space, intimate normal-lens proximity, or measured longer observation for power.
- Height and position: occupy table, workshop, doorway, stage, field, or corridor at participant height.
- Movement and focus: track confidently with entrances or labor, circle or push only as control shifts, and retain contextual focus.
- Witness role: a fascinated participant in the duel, not a detached institution; reject empty long-take display.

## Composition and Spatial Fingerprint
- Visual center: hold two competing wills in one social or work relation.
- Layers and obstruction: use tables, tools, doorways, mirrors, corridors, workers, and landscape to support dependency.
- Blocking and geometry: let one person invade, withdraw, mirror, or dominate the other's working territory.
- Information control: show the power exchange while leaving affection and motive contradictory.

## Blocking and Story Translation
- Moment: choose a ritual just before control reverses.
- Character action: serve, inspect, rehearse, interrupt, repair, eat, or refuse.
- Visual center: bind two wills through one task or object.
- Blocking: use encroachment and changing distance.
- Environment: make workshop, table, field, or institution carry labor and status.

## Capture Texture
Use substantial skin, cloth, wood, oil, food, machinery, and source-rich exposure. Avoid prestige-film polish and decorative period texture.

## Common Misreadings
- Using long takes without a power duel.
- Making every scene loudly confrontational.
- Applying warm period grading.
- Treating eccentricity as character depth.
- Styling labor and fabric as luxury advertising.

## Nearest-Neighbor Contrast
Unlike Martin Scorsese, Paul Thomas Anderson sustains bilateral power negotiation: richer stable source light, period/material color, measured tracking or medium-wide staging, and territory shared by two obsessive wills rather than volatile practical shifts, street color, implicated social camera, and one performer exposed by a wider judging network.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor
Use rich source-naturalistic contrast, substantial material color, confident participant camera, two competing wills bound by a task, changing bodily distance, and a work or social ritual approaching reversal.

## Default Iconic Anchor
Reselect the beat before control reverses; keep both wills and the shared task in one spatial relation, let the camera move only with encroachment or withdrawal, and use source-rich skin and material texture without prestige warmth.

## Scene Translation
- Scene: a tailor fits a mayor's coat after learning the contract will be canceled.
- Translation: the tailor tightens one sleeve while the mayor continues dictating terms; a measured workshop track keeps scissors, assistants, mirror, and shrinking distance active under north-window light and one work lamp.
