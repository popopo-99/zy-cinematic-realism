# Shunji Iwai

## Identity
- Chinese name: 岩井俊二
- Region: Japan
- Representative works: *Love Letter*, *All About Lily Chou-Chou*, *Hana and Alice*
- Best suited for: youth, memory, seasonal transition, private connection.

## Core Logic
Choose a small afterimage of change rather than a declaration. Emotion lives in hesitation, unattended objects, and the distance between people.

## Story and Character
Use departures, school corridors after class, letters, or a routine ending. Let feeling appear through sorting, waiting, or looking away.

## Camera and Space
Observe at a gentle remove through doors, glass, or room depth; keep room for weather and empty space. Movement is restrained, never a music-video flourish.

## Light and Color
Use plausible window light, overcast softness, or a practical interior lamp. Seasonal color is quiet, not pastel decoration.

## Best Use Cases
Graduation, delayed recognition, siblings, quiet friendship, remembered rooms.

## Common Misreadings
- Forced dreamy haze.
- Constant petals or snow.
- Fashion posing.
- Decorative overexposure.

## Model-Facing Style Anchor

- Explicit director token: Shunji Iwai
- Representative film anchors: *Love Letter*, *All About Lily Chou-Chou*, *Hana and Alice*
- Strong visual grammar: intimate normal-lens or gently distant observation; vulnerable everyday gestures separated by weather, glass, or rooms; natural or slightly unstable available light; pale seasonal material color without a decorative wash; softness motivated by exposure, distance, or air; tactile, imperfect capture; memory and emotional residue rather than plot explanation.
- Do not reduce to: cherry blossoms, white dresses, schoolgirl imagery, soft-focus haze, or a generic dreamy filter.
- Prompt insertion: Director reference: Shunji Iwai, with broad visual reference points in *Love Letter*, *All About Lily Chou-Chou*, and *Hana and Alice*. Use an intimate, season-sensitive, memory-fragmented visual language: vulnerable everyday gestures, natural or slightly unstable available light, gentle observational distance, youthful emotional residue, quiet spatial separation, and softness motivated by weather, glass, distance, or exposure rather than a decorative dreamy filter.

## Default Iconic Anchor

Director and visual reference: Shunji Iwai, drawing strongly from the visual language associated with Love Letter, All About Lily Chou-Chou, and Hana and Alice. Reselect the scene around a vulnerable everyday gesture and lingering emotional residue; use intimate normal-lens observation, quiet separation through weather, glass, or distance, natural or slightly unstable available light, pale material color, focus softened only by exposure or air, and imperfect tactile capture rather than a dreamy filter. Keep skin, fabric, and ordinary spaces unpolished and selectively visible.

## Scene Translation
- story beat: a student packs the final notebook after everyone leaves.
- character action: she pauses over a name inside the cover.
- camera position: from the corridor, framed by an open door.
- lighting: late window light across used desks.
- avoid: music-video gloss, magical particles, posed nostalgia.
