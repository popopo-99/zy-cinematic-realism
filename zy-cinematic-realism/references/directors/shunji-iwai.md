# Shunji Iwai

## Identity
- Chinese name: 岩井俊二
- Region: Japan
- Representative works: *Love Letter*, *All About Lily Chou-Chou*, *Hana and Alice*
- Best suited for: youth, memory, seasonal transition, private connection.

## Core Logic
Choose a small afterimage of change rather than a declaration. Emotion lives in hesitation, unattended objects, and the distance between people.

## Story and Character
Use departures, school corridors after class, letters, or a routine ending. Let feeling appear through sorting, waiting, or looking away.

## Camera and Space
Observe at a gentle remove through doors, glass, or room depth; keep room for weather and empty space. Movement is restrained, never a music-video flourish.

## Light and Color
Use plausible window light, overcast softness, or a practical interior lamp. Seasonal color is quiet, not pastel decoration.

## Best Use Cases
Graduation, delayed recognition, siblings, quiet friendship, remembered rooms.

## Common Misreadings
- Forced dreamy haze.
- Constant petals or snow.
- Fashion posing.
- Decorative overexposure.

## Scene Translation
- story beat: a student packs the final notebook after everyone leaves.
- character action: she pauses over a name inside the cover.
- camera position: from the corridor, framed by an open door.
- lighting: late window light across used desks.
- avoid: music-video gloss, magical particles, posed nostalgia.
