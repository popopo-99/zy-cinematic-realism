# Story Source Ledger / 故事来源台账

Use only when multiple scenes, nonlinear chronology, source revisions, or a requested handoff need traceability. A short scene can keep these distinctions internally. This is a compact source and dependency record, not automatic storage, a complete plot database, or a replacement for the Continuity Bible or Scene Master.

## Minimum Record

Include only supported fields needed for the project:

- **Source / revision:** supplied file or text identifier and actual version label, if given. Distinguish separate sources; do not invent an original draft date.
- **Read coverage:** actual scenes/pages/paragraphs or transcript range read, plus unavailable or unresolved portions. Record file-page and printed-page locators separately when verified. A summary does not certify the original screenplay's contents.
- **Scene ID / source locator:** a stable internal identifier, supplied heading or scene number, and a usable location in the source. Label an internal ID as internal; it is not an original screenplay number. When a revision inserts or renumbers scenes, map corresponding scenes by their content and preserve existing internal IDs when appropriate.
- **Facts and state:** explicit event, characters, time order, location, relationship changes, and before/after wardrobe and prop states. Separate reading order from story chronology when the text supports that distinction.
- **Knowledge / reveal state:** what each relevant character knows and what the audience has been shown at this point. Keep unresolved truth separate from a character's claim or belief.
- **Interpretations / proposals / acceptance:** distinguish the narrative reading, new visual choices, user-accepted decisions, and unresolved alternatives. An assistant suggestion is not a lock.
- **Frame dependencies:** frame ID when useful, source scene/beat, relevant character knowledge, wardrobe/prop/geography state, and accepted art-direction decisions used by that frame.
- **Result status:** prompt ready, generated, inspected, user accepted, or reported failure, using only observed or reported evidence.

Use source locators or short supporting descriptions rather than duplicating the whole script. Show the ledger only when requested or needed to explain a consequential dependency; otherwise keep the final output focused on the user's task.

## Responsibility and Authority

- The **ledger** records source evidence, interpretation/proposal status, reading coverage, and dependencies.
- The **Continuity Bible** owns accepted scene/story continuity and accepted shared art-direction constraints; keep wardrobe, props, geography, and current knowledge/state there.
- The **Scene Master** remains the canonical facts and resolved visual decisions for the current frame.
- A **Decode Card** owns visual grammar observed from supplied images, never unobserved screenplay expression or story facts.

Source facts enter the Scene Master as fixed facts for faithful development. Explicit user adaptation may change them, with the change recorded. OPEN candidate choices may be completed for the requested output; do not relabel them as authored facts or accepted Bible rules. Newer explicit user instructions outrank older project preferences. An acceptance decision applies to the frame or choice actually accepted, not every attached alternative.

## Source Revisions and Impact

1. Identify the actual revised source and changed facts within the available text. Preserve the prior source identity and map affected scene IDs. If only an excerpt was supplied, do not claim the rest of the screenplay was checked.
2. Follow dependencies, not just scene numbers. A changed prop owner can affect later hand occupancy; a changed reveal can affect earlier suspense and later reactions; clothing damage or wetness can persist; a setting/time change can affect geography and light across scenes.
3. Classify affected prompts/frames as **Still applies**, **Needs review**, or **Needs recompile**, with the specific dependency. A dialogue wording change alone need not invalidate an image when the visible action and information remain the same. Unknown downstream material stays unverified rather than being assumed unchanged.
4. Update the relevant Bible state and current Scene Master for the requested work. Preserve unrelated accepted facts and visual decisions. Recompile only the requested affected outputs; an impact analysis does not authorize automatic whole-project regeneration or discard user-accepted images.
5. Keep image status distinct from prompt status. Revising a prompt does not mean an old image was repaired, replaced, or reaccepted.

## Continue in Another Conversation

When requested, include the source version and read coverage, current scene/frame IDs, relevant facts and knowledge/state, complete accepted visual rules, unresolved proposals, and revision impact in the existing [Project Handoff Card](project-handoff.md). Identify which source excerpts or images must be supplied again for faithful reanalysis, editing, or comparison. Continue a text-only task from sufficiently complete supplied facts without asking for irrelevant attachments or claiming access to unavailable originals.
