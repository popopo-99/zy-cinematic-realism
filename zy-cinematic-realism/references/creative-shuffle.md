# Creative Shuffle

Use when the user wants an unexpected direction or has no starting inspiration. Shuffle causal grammar, not isolated adjectives.

## Draw

Choose one card from each layer:

- **Story Card:** Anticipation, Waiting, Aftermath, Interrupted Routine, Departure, Near Encounter, Procedural Work, Private Observation, Delayed Discovery, or Return.
- **Camera Card:** select a physically possible witness method from [cinematography-cards.md](cinematography-cards.md) or use Peripheral Witness when an obstructed edge view best serves the scene.
- **Style Card:** select one compatible card from [style-cards.md](style-cards.md).

Apply all three to one Scene Master. A card changes its assigned grammar; it does not override user-fixed facts.

## Story Card Meanings

- **Anticipation:** a preparation or held breath before a known action.
- **Waiting:** time accumulates through repeated small behavior and environmental evidence.
- **Aftermath:** consequence is visible after the main event has passed.
- **Interrupted Routine:** an ordinary procedure stops because of one concrete disturbance.
- **Departure:** a threshold, packing action, last look, or separation is underway.
- **Near Encounter:** two paths almost meet; distance and withheld awareness carry the beat.
- **Procedural Work:** competence and repetition reveal character through tools and sequence.
- **Private Observation:** a character watches without joining or being acknowledged.
- **Delayed Discovery:** evidence is present before the character fully recognizes it.
- **Return:** a familiar place is re-entered after an absence, with one changed condition.

## Constraints

- Preserve all fixed facts.
- Keep cause and effect legible.
- Introduce at most one dominant unusual visual premise unless the user requests more.
- Maintain a physically possible camera and motivated source light.
- Do not randomize director names.
- Reject combinations whose cards demand contradictory camera, light, or story logic; redraw only the conflicting card.

Output `Combination`, the three selected cards, and `Final Prompt` unless the user asks for prompt-only output.
