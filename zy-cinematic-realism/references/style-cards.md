# Original Style Cards

Style Cards alter story, blocking, space, light, color, and material grammar. They are not prompt keyword bundles. Select one card, translate its decisions into the Scene Master, and never paste the card verbatim into a model prompt.

## Observational Social Realism / 观察式社会现实

- **Best for:** labor, public space, family pressure, and ordinary institutions.
- **Story engine:** a social condition appears through routine rather than explanation.
- **Preferred moment:** waiting, procedural work, or unresolved aftermath.
- **Blocking:** people occupy practical positions and rarely perform emotion outwardly.
- **Camera role:** patient witness at human distance, sometimes limited by architecture.
- **Light / contrast:** available sources with uneven reach and unprotected dead areas.
- **Color / exposure:** restrained local color; skin and walls accept imperfect exposure.
- **Spatial logic:** environment and institutional geometry share importance with people.
- **Material behavior:** wear follows repeated use, maintenance, weather, and class conditions.
- **Reject / anti-clichés:** misery spectacle, sentimental poverty, heroic worker portrait, beautified decay.

## Domestic Aftermath / 家庭余波

- **Best for:** arguments, departures, secrets, exhaustion, and changed household routines.
- **Story engine:** the event is over; displaced objects and incomplete tasks carry consequence.
- **Preferred moment:** seconds or hours after someone leaves or stops speaking.
- **Blocking:** bodies separate into different zones; hands continue a small unnecessary task.
- **Camera role:** quiet observer from a doorway, hallway, or seated room position.
- **Light / contrast:** window or practical light falls unevenly across lived-in rooms.
- **Color / exposure:** familiar domestic colors lose saturation in shadow or late light.
- **Spatial logic:** thresholds, empty chairs, counters, and adjoining rooms reveal relationships.
- **Material behavior:** cups cool, fabric creases, food dries, water marks remain.
- **Reject / anti-clichés:** tearful close-up, perfect interior styling, symbolic object overload, melodramatic backlight.

## Nocturnal Procedure / 夜间程序

- **Best for:** maintenance, investigation, transport, medical work, and security routines.
- **Story engine:** a repeated task continues while fatigue or hidden risk accumulates.
- **Preferred moment:** a check, handoff, pause, or small anomaly during work.
- **Blocking:** tools and work sequence determine posture and distance.
- **Camera role:** observes from a useful but non-privileged workplace position.
- **Light / contrast:** practical pools separated by shadow; intermittent exterior spill is secondary.
- **Color / exposure:** source-specific contamination without global teal-orange grading.
- **Spatial logic:** corridors, bays, vehicles, counters, and access boundaries organize the frame.
- **Material behavior:** oil, glass, metal, reflective tape, skin, and uniforms respond differently.
- **Reject / anti-clichés:** generic neon noir, tactical hero pose, decorative smoke, spotless equipment.

## Memory in Transit / 旅途记忆

- **Best for:** buses, trains, cars, ferries, stations, and temporary separation.
- **Story engine:** movement outside contrasts with a character mentally fixed on a recent event.
- **Preferred moment:** between departure and arrival, after conversation has ended.
- **Blocking:** seated or standing bodies relate through aisles, windows, and unoccupied space.
- **Camera role:** fellow passenger or platform witness, never omniscient nostalgia.
- **Light / contrast:** passing sources, window reflections, station spill, or dawn transition.
- **Color / exposure:** mixed travel light with slight exposure drift caused by movement.
- **Spatial logic:** layered glass and repeated seats compress private and public space.
- **Material behavior:** condensation, vibration, worn upholstery, tickets, and luggage remain causal.
- **Reject / anti-clichés:** dreamy blur everywhere, travel-ad beauty, arbitrary lens flare, perfect longing pose.

## Historical Material Realism / 历史材质现实

- **Best for:** period labor, domestic life, institutions, travel, and non-heroic history.
- **Story engine:** history appears through what people must physically do with period objects.
- **Preferred moment:** preparation, maintenance, waiting, or consequence rather than pageantry.
- **Blocking:** clothing construction, tools, furniture, and etiquette constrain movement.
- **Camera role:** plausible observer within period architecture, not a museum display.
- **Light / contrast:** sources available to the era; shadow remains materially dense.
- **Color / exposure:** dyes, paints, metals, smoke, and daylight retain period-specific limits.
- **Spatial logic:** doors, hearths, streets, workshops, and transport remain usable and topologically clear.
- **Material behavior:** patina follows age and handling, not decorative “vintage” treatment.
- **Reject / anti-clichés:** costume-pageant posing, fantasy cleanliness, generic sepia, anachronistic fixtures.

## Quiet Suspense / 静默悬念

- **Best for:** withheld information, near encounters, delayed discovery, and ambiguous threat.
- **Story engine:** one ordinary action continues while a second fact changes its meaning.
- **Preferred moment:** before recognition or just after a barely noticed disturbance.
- **Blocking:** characters face different information zones; eye-lines do not explain everything.
- **Camera role:** knows slightly more or less than the character, from a credible boundary.
- **Light / contrast:** source hierarchy hides one relevant zone without theatrical blackness.
- **Color / exposure:** restrained, with attention controlled by exposure rather than accent color.
- **Spatial logic:** corridors, reflections, doors, and depth create delayed information.
- **Material behavior:** small movement in curtains, liquid, dust, or loose objects signals consequence.
- **Reject / anti-clichés:** Dutch angle, obvious villain silhouette, horror blue light, jump-scare framing.

## Weather-Borne Intimacy / 天气承载的亲密

- **Best for:** restrained relationships shaped by rain, snow, heat, wind, or humidity.
- **Story engine:** weather changes how people share shelter, distance, clothing, and objects.
- **Preferred moment:** just before shelter, during a pause, or after weather has passed.
- **Blocking:** bodies negotiate umbrellas, coats, heat, wet seats, or limited cover.
- **Camera role:** stays within the same weather condition rather than observing from perfect safety.
- **Light / contrast:** weather modifies real sources, visibility, and surface highlights.
- **Color / exposure:** atmospheric loss and color contamination follow distance and moisture.
- **Spatial logic:** shelter boundaries and exposed zones structure emotional distance.
- **Material behavior:** fabric weight, skin, glass, pavement, breath, and hair respond physically.
- **Reject / anti-clichés:** romantic rain machine, universal glossy wetness, decorative fog, music-video embrace.

## Landscape of Duty / 职责之地

- **Best for:** remote work, patrol, agriculture, infrastructure, and care across large spaces.
- **Story engine:** obligation continues despite scale, isolation, or slow environmental change.
- **Preferred moment:** inspection, return, preparation, or a pause between tasks.
- **Blocking:** people remain small and purposefully placed relative to routes and equipment.
- **Camera role:** distant witness whose position respects terrain and access.
- **Light / contrast:** broad environmental source with limited local practical light when justified.
- **Color / exposure:** landscape dominates color; human elements do not receive advertising separation.
- **Spatial logic:** paths, barriers, horizons, and work zones explain movement.
- **Material behavior:** wind, dust, water, rust, vegetation, and clothing show sustained exposure.
- **Reject / anti-clichés:** tourism panorama, drone spectacle by default, lone-hero silhouette, epic grading.

## Accidental Editorial / 偶发时尚感

- **Best for:** ordinary behavior that briefly forms a visually tense arrangement.
- **Story engine:** an unposed event accidentally aligns clothing, gesture, and architecture.
- **Preferred moment:** mid-adjustment, waiting, crossing, or handling an object.
- **Blocking:** asymmetry and body angles arise from action, never runway presentation.
- **Camera role:** catches the alignment from a plausible passing position.
- **Light / contrast:** existing light creates graphic separation without campaign polish.
- **Color / exposure:** wardrobe color may lead once, while skin and surroundings remain natural.
- **Spatial logic:** lines and negative space emerge from the location rather than a set.
- **Material behavior:** fabric folds, weight, weather, and movement stay visible.
- **Reject / anti-clichés:** fashion posing, campaign lighting, luxury-commercial polish, perfect wardrobe display.

## Urban Intrusion / 都市异物现实主义

- **Best for:** one unusual person, garment, or object inside a contemporary ordinary city.
- **Story engine:** only one thing is unusual; daily systems continue around it.
- **Preferred moment:** commuting, parking, waiting, maintenance, or unnoticed passage.
- **Blocking:** the unusual element follows the same gravity, access, and social distance as everything else.
- **Camera role:** observes as a credible passerby or peripheral witness.
- **Light / contrast:** existing urban sources illuminate the anomaly without “reveal” lighting.
- **Color / exposure:** the city stays ordinary; the anomaly does not recolor the world.
- **Spatial logic:** parking bays, elevators, offices, roads, and barriers remain fully realistic.
- **Material behavior:** unusual materials respond to concrete, rain, dust, and fluorescent light physically.
- **Reject / anti-clichés:** full fantasy world, cyberpunk transformation, theatrical crowd reactions, worldwide visual anomaly.

## Infrastructural Sublime / 基础设施崇高

- **Best for:** tunnels, stormwater systems, power stations, airports, ports, dams, and industrial facilities.
- **Story engine:** human procedure continues inside a system too large to perceive at once.
- **Preferred moment:** inspection, delay, handoff, or distant movement through scale.
- **Blocking:** people remain small and tied to walkways, vehicles, controls, or safety zones.
- **Camera role:** stands at an accessible service position, not an impossible aerial viewpoint.
- **Light / contrast:** industrial practicals and environmental spill reveal only part of the system.
- **Color / exposure:** functional paint, concrete, metal, weather, and safety colors dominate.
- **Spatial logic:** repeating structural units and access routes establish scale.
- **Material behavior:** mass, moisture, corrosion, dust, vibration, and heat have physical consequences.
- **Reject / anti-clichés:** generic sci-fi megastructure, impossible architecture, drone spectacle by default, tiny hero worship.

## Peripheral Witness / 边缘目击

- **Best for:** private gestures, social distance, uncertainty, and events seen without access.
- **Story engine:** partial access makes the viewer reconstruct what matters.
- **Preferred moment:** before acknowledgement, during a quiet exchange, or just after separation.
- **Blocking:** action remains readable through partial occlusion and off-axis relationships.
- **Camera role:** behind a vehicle, through glass, beside a pillar, inside a doorway, past a curtain, or around a corridor corner.
- **Light / contrast:** the boundary has its own exposure and reflection behavior.
- **Color / exposure:** foreground obstruction does not become a decorative color frame.
- **Spatial logic:** occlusion follows a real witness position and preserves topology.
- **Material behavior:** glass, paint, fabric, or vehicle surfaces affect clarity plausibly.
- **Reject / anti-clichés:** perfect front-row camera, clean hero coverage, pasted-on obstruction, unreadable primary action.

## Quiet Catastrophe / 静默灾变

- **Best for:** major events represented through changed routines and small consequences.
- **Story engine:** the catastrophe has occurred; ordinary behavior now reveals its scale.
- **Preferred moment:** aftermath, return, cleanup, inventory, or waiting for information.
- **Blocking:** people perform limited tasks rather than display panic.
- **Camera role:** observes consequence at ground level and human scale.
- **Light / contrast:** weather and remaining practical sources define visibility.
- **Color / exposure:** avoid poster-grade danger colors; let environmental change carry tone.
- **Spatial logic:** one blocked route, absent object, damaged zone, or altered procedure anchors the event.
- **Material behavior:** debris, water, dust, ash, or breakage follows a credible cause.
- **Reject / anti-clichés:** explosion, heroic running, mass panic, disaster-poster composition.

## Ritual Realism / 现实仪式感

- **Best for:** putting on equipment, checking machinery, closing a shop, removing armor, packing tools, or preparing food.
- **Story engine:** repeated order reveals care, duty, fatigue, or transition.
- **Preferred moment:** one precise step before completion.
- **Blocking:** hands, tools, surfaces, and sequence determine posture.
- **Camera role:** respects the work area and watches without monumentalizing it.
- **Light / contrast:** task light or available source prioritizes the active step.
- **Color / exposure:** functional color and controlled highlights clarify material state.
- **Spatial logic:** objects occupy known places; absence or displacement has meaning.
- **Material behavior:** touch, weight, heat, residue, and wear reveal repetition.
- **Reject / anti-clichés:** unjustified ceremonial fantasy, hero pose, slow-motion cliché encoded as a still, sacred-symbol overload.

## Material Tension / 材质冲突

- **Best for:** scenes whose visual center is a physical relationship such as metal/wet concrete, silk/stone, skin/glass, porcelain/mist, or oil/cloth.
- **Story engine:** action brings two materials into contact or contrast.
- **Preferred moment:** touch, cleaning, impact aftermath, transfer, or separation.
- **Blocking:** hands and objects reveal weight, temperature, friction, and risk.
- **Camera role:** close enough to read the relationship while retaining its spatial cause.
- **Light / contrast:** source angle reveals different reflection, absorption, or translucency.
- **Color / exposure:** color follows material and source, not decorative grading.
- **Spatial logic:** surrounding space explains why the materials meet.
- **Material behavior:** each surface obeys its own roughness, moisture, deformation, and thermal state.
- **Reject / anti-clichés:** arbitrary texture adjectives, everything glossy, impossible reflection, material properties contradicting light.

## Transitional Light / 过渡时刻

- **Best for:** before sunrise, after sunset, rain just stopped, alarm just ended, a door just opened, lights just switched off, or cloud cover breaking.
- **Story engine:** a source state changes while the action continues.
- **Preferred moment:** the first or last seconds of the transition.
- **Blocking:** characters react practically to changing visibility or temperature.
- **Camera role:** occupies a position where both old and new source states are spatially legible.
- **Light / contrast:** one dominant transition with causal shadow and highlight movement.
- **Color / exposure:** mixed color exists only where overlapping sources reach.
- **Spatial logic:** doorways, windows, clouds, fixtures, or moving cover explain the change.
- **Material behavior:** wetness, glass, skin, fabric, and metal respond at different rates.
- **Reject / anti-clichés:** permanent golden-hour look, multiple decorative sources, unmotivated rim light, universal glow.
